## Menu

- Tuna — seared tuna with vegetables and soy-oyster sauce (signature)
- Curried Lobster Bisque, Pan-Fried Calamari, Tuna Trio
- Grilled catch of the day (wahoo/mahi/dorade depending on the 08:00–11:00 landing)
- Old/New World wine list, cocktails, dessert “Lime with Friends”

The most photographed oceanfront restaurant in Kralendijk: come for sunset (18:30–19:00). 200 m from the dinghy dock at Karel's Bar — easy on foot from the moorings. Two contracts with local line-fishing crews for exclusive catch ★★★. Book a waterfront table.
