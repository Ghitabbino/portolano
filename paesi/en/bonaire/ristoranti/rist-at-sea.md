## Notes

Intimate restaurant opposite Dive Friends @ Dive Inn — ideal for a quiet dinner after checking in on the moorings (Kralendijk 12°09.1′ N). Ingredients are limited on the island: the surprise menu is the best way to taste the best available that day. Next door to Capriccio (same street) for easy comparison.
