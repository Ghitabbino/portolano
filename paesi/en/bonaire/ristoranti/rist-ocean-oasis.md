## Notes

The largest beach club on Bonaire, 3.5 km from the Kralendijk moorings (7 min by taxi, 15 min by bike) — ideal for a beach lunch or dinner with an evening DJ set. Book loungers/cabana online by 12:00. Also open for breakfast — handy after a night on the mooring. Near Brass Boer (same Punt Vierkant area) for a southern gourmet tour.
