## Notes

The resort restaurant that manages the moorings — extremely convenient for crews on the moorings: resort dinghy dock 1 min away, then dinner without a taxi. Ideal for check-in day (arrival → mooring → dinner). Book a waterfront table for sunset. Resort access is courtesy for marina/mooring guests.
