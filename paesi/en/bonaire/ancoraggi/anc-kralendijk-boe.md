# Kralendijk Mooring Field — Only Overnight Moorings for Visitors {#anc-kralendijk-boe}

[← All Moorings](../08-ancoraggi.md)
**12°09′21″ N 068°16′49″ W** ★★★★ — centre of field (official limits 12°09′26″ N 068°17′05″ W and 12°09′04″ N 068°16′25″ W) · WGS84

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Depth** | Sand and protected coral beneath the field; no published sounding — depth varies along the west-coast strip (check sounder) | 
| **Holding / Anchoring** | **Moorings only — anchoring PROHIBITED** everywhere in the BNMP (fine up to US$ 2,800 + 1 month) ★★★★★ |
| **Wind / Shelter** | Leeward west coast in the ENE trades: flat water and good shelter; exposed to N/NW swell with winter cold fronts — check the local forecast ★★★ |
| **Hazards** | Continuous fringing reef poorly marked; tender/dive-boat traffic on the yellow day moorings; frequent dinghy/engine theft at Green dock/Kralendijk moorings at night → chain + bar-lock, hoist the tender ★★★ |
| **Crowding** | **Very busy in high season** (Dec–Apr): first-come first-served, call Harbour Village before entry; 30–51 two-tone moorings (white/red, yellow pendant, rugby-ball shape) |
| **Moorings / Rules** | **Two-tone overnight for visitors**: US$ 35/night, max 18 m (≈55–58 ft), one boat per mooring (max 3 tenders <4 m), soft line ≥6 m (do not tie directly), 5 km/h in the field, 50 m from other boats, holding tank mandatory, stay = visa duration, no commercial activity ★★★★★ |
| **Ashore** | Central Kralendijk 200–600 m on foot from the dinghy dock at **Karel's Bar** or from the Customs/Immigration jetty (Fort Oranje, Plasa Wilhelmina 3); Harbour Village Marina to the north handles allocation/payment (VHF 17/12, tel +599 717 7419, dockmaster@harbourvillage.com); pump-out included (or US$40 per pump-out) |
