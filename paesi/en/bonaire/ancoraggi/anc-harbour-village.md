**12°09′44″ N 068°17′19″ W** ★★★★ — basin entrance (WGS84, Navily/Harbour Village)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Depth** | ~4.5 m at the entrance; sheltered inner basin with dredged sand/coral bottom |
| **Holding / Anchoring** | — (resort marina, not an anchorage: **anchoring prohibited** in the BNMP) |
| **Wind / Shelter** | Excellent shelter from ENE trades (basin north of Kralendijk); exposed to N/NW swell with cold fronts — check forecast before entry ★★ |
| **Hazards** | Resort/charter traffic at the entrance; bottom drops away quickly outside the basin; reef south of the entrance |
| **Moorings / Rules** | **Official manager of STINAPA overnight moorings** for visitors: allocation first-come via VHF 17 (and 12), payment on site US$ 35/night, briefing on mooring use (line ≥6 m, max 18 m, one boat/mooring) ★★★★★; Customs/Immigration 0.5 NM to the south (Fort Oranje) — sole port of entry |
| **Ashore** | 5★ full-service resort: **60 berths** up to 60 ft + mega-yacht dock to **240 ft (Ambar Point)**; water US$0.10/gal, power US$0.75/kWh (127–130V 30A / 230–240V 50A), diesel/petrol + bunker >10,000 l via Curoil, pump-out, WiFi, showers, laundry, parking, security included; bookings [Request Slip](https://www.harbourvillage.com/bonaire-marina) |
| **Contacts & Hours** | Tel **+599 717 7419** · WhatsApp **+599 701 7500** · dockmaster@harbourvillage.com · VHF **17** (mooring) / **12** (availability) / 16 listening watch · Mon–Fri 08:30–12:00 & 13:30–17:00, Sat 08:00–13:00 ★★★★ |
| **Crowding** | Medium-high in high season (Dec–Apr): book before the passage; check-out at noon, payment in advance |
