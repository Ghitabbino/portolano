# 00 — Entry, Documents & Visas

**Last checked: 28/08/2026**

## Status

Bonaire is a **special municipality (openbaar lichaam) of the Netherlands** in the **Dutch Caribbean BES** (Bonaire, St. Eustatius and Saba) — part of the Kingdom of the Netherlands but **outside the EU and outside the Schengen Area**, with entry and customs rules distinct from Aruba, Curaçao and Sint Maarten (constituent countries of the Kingdom). BES rules are neither EU nor Schengen rules.

## Important Note for Arrival by Sea

- **People checks** (Immigration/IND + KMar) are **separate from the boat formalities** (Customs / Belastingdienst Caribisch Nederland): even with a valid EU passport you must complete **mandatory clearance for the boat** on entry and on departure → see [01 — Clearance](01-clearance.md).
- **Only port of entry: Kralendijk** — Customs and Immigration share the same building at Fort Oranje / Plasa Wilhelmina (Harbour Office area). **The entire island is a Marine Park: anchoring is forbidden**, use only **STINAPA moorings** (overnight two-tone moorings) or a marina berth. [Ocean Posse — Bonaire](https://oceanposse.com/bonaire/) ★★★; [STINAPA — Mooring](https://stinapabonaire.org/rules-and-regulations/mooring/) ★★★★★
- Have ready for each person: passport, **crew list**, outbound ticket / proof of onward passage, address of stay (mooring number/marina), proof of means; for the boat: **registration/nationality, proof of ownership, crew list and the mandatory outbound document (zarpe/clearance) from the previous port** — without proof of the port of departure entry may be refused. **SailClear** pre-notification has been expected by Customs since 2023: there is no computer on the quay, file it **before departure** at [sailclear.com](https://www.sailclear.com). Cruising sources aggregate ★★★; [CruisersWiki / Ocean Posse] ★★

## Beyond 90 Days? (Italian / EU and Other Nationals)

| Situation | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Tourist stay beyond 90/180 | A **residence permit** is required. Apply to **IND Caribisch Nederland — Bonaire, Kaya Gresia 11, Kralendijk** (information desk Mon–Thu 08:00–11:30; collection Mon–Thu 13:30–16:00 Fri 08:00–11:30; tel +599 715 8330, IND@rijksdienstCN.com, info line Mon–Thu 08:00–12:00) | [RijksdienstCN — Contact IND Bonaire](https://english.rijksdienstcn.com/immigration-naturalisation/contact) ★★★★★ |
| Dutch/US nationals beyond 180/365 (or 90/365 if working) | A ***Verklaring van rechtswege* (Declaration of Admittance by Law)** is required by appointment at IND | [RijksdienstCN — Tourist stay](https://english.rijksdienstcn.com/immigration-naturalisation/tourist-stay) ★★★★★ |
| Work / internship during the stay | Even if you start work after arriving as a tourist, the **90/365** cap immediately applies to Dutch/US nationals and a work permit is required; for other foreign nationals a specific permit is required — **work is not permitted on a tourist entry alone** | [RijksdienstCN — Tourist stay](https://english.rijksdienstcn.com/immigration-naturalisation/tourist-stay) ★★★★★ |
| Overstay | Possible penalties: fine, removal, re-entry ban (decision by KMar/IND) — the period may already have been shortened at the border | [RijksdienstCN — Travel with non-Dutch passport](https://english.rijksdienstcn.com/justice--security/royal-netherlands-marechaussee/travel-with-a-non-dutch-passport) ★★★★★ |

In practice: with an Italian passport you enter without a visa but **within a 90/180 window across the entire BES** and with checks on return ticket/means/accommodation. To stay longer or to work you need a local IND application — indicative processing 2–4 weeks according to a secondary non-governmental source (**DATA MISSING** for official Bonaire processing time).

## Vaccinations

No mandatory vaccination for arrivals direct from Europe/USA. **Yellow fever vaccination required only if you come from or have transited >12 h in a country at risk of transmission** (Amazon basin, sub-Saharan Africa — WHO list). In that case an **International Certificate valid for life since 2016 is required for travellers aged ≥9 months**, to be shown on entry; vaccination at least **10 days before** arrival.
