# 05 — Seasonality & Weather

**Last checked: 28/08/2026**

## Climate

- Mean temperature **~27–30 °C** (max 30–32 °C, min 24–27 °C); **semi-arid BSh/BS** climate in the southern Dry Zone, moderate to fresh E/ENE trades, humidity ~75%. ★★★★★
- Average rainfall **350–450 mm/year** over ~65 days (peak Oct–Jan), highly variable; sea temperature **27 °C** mean (25.9 °C Feb–Mar, 28.2 °C Sep–Oct); diurnal range exceeds seasonal range. ★★★★★

## Seasons

| Period | Characteristics |
|---|---|
| **February – May** | Dry season, steady trades and mostly clear skies: **best time for cruising** |
| **October – January** | **Rainy season** with brief showers in the morning/early evening; peak Oct–Dec; Jul–Sep transitional |

★★★

## Hurricane Preparedness (Official ★★★★)

Bonaire has no French-style cyclone plan; follow **KNMI Caribisch Nederland** and **Meteo Curaçao (MDC)** warnings via [knmidc.org](https://www.knmidc.org/weather/bonaire/) and VHF; the local disaster committee activates an annual plan; no berth is guaranteed 100% in a cyclone.

## Hurricanes

Season **1 June–30 November**, peak mid-Aug to mid-Oct. Bonaire sits on the **southern edge of the hurricane belt (12°N)**: a cyclone within 100 NM **~1 in 11 years**; a direct hit historically **~1 in 38.5 years**; northern fringes possible. Past events: Ivan (2004, 65 NM north Cat.4), Felix (2007, 30 NM north), Bonnie (2022, Code Red 3–5" rain), Beryl (2024, 121 km north of Rincon). [meteo.cw ★★★★★; KNMI ★★★★★; bonaire.com ★★★]

## Local Winds

- Trades from **E/ENE 12–20 kt**, mean ~7–8 m/s stronger in the dry/transition season and lighter in the rainy season; acceleration gusts between the ABC Islands and in the lee of low ground. ★★★★★
- Saharan dust (SAL) and Venezuelan upwelling suppress cloud; high pressure can bring E–NW seas 4–7 ft with small-craft advisories (≥6 Bft) on exposed coasts. ★★★★

## Typical Weather Windows

Short passages Bonaire–Curaçao (~35 NM) or Bonaire–Aruba (~105 NM) are workable with trades ≤18–20 kt, best at dawn; channels: confused seas — avoid >20 kt. Long passages towards Colombia/Venezuela: wait for a stable post-tropical-wave window. ★★
