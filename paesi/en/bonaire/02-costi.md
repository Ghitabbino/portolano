# 02 — Cost of Living

**Last updated: 28/08/2026**

Currency: **USD (US dollar)** — Bonaire is a Dutch special municipality (BES) but only USD has circulated since 2011. Price level: ~90% imported goods; groceries **~+40%** vs the European Netherlands, tourist restaurants expensive in marina/dive-resort areas. ★★★

> ⚠️ The items in this list are the same for every country in the pilot. Where a figure has not yet been found from the sources we write **DATA MISSING**: it will be sought at the monthly check (see [00 Index](00-indice.md)).

## Cash & Payments

USD is the sole legal currency (ANG/EUR not accepted). Visa/Mastercard widely accepted in marinas/supermarkets/tourist restaurants; cash USD for markets, taxis, water taxis, moorings (rangers afloat) and tips. ATMs in Kralendijk (Kaya Grandi) and at the airport; Curoil pumps 24/7 by card. Small notes recommended. ★★★
