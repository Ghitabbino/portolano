# 01 — Yacht Customs Clearance

**Last checked: 28/08/2026** · Main sources: Belastingdienst Caribisch Nederland (Customs CN) ★★★★★, RijksdienstCN/IND ★★★★★, STINAPA ★★★★★, Cruising sources aggregate/Ocean Posse/CruisersWiki ★★★

## Who Must Clear

All leisure craft, **any flag and nationality**, that:
- **enter** BES waters (Bonaire) coming from a foreign port/territorial sea or the high seas;
- **depart** for a foreign port/sea or the high seas.
BES residents returning from foreign passages must also report. Movement within **Bonaire ↔ Saba/St. Eustatius** is inside the same BES territory but, after a passage through international waters, reporting on entry is still required. [Belastingdienst CN — Douane Caribisch Nederland](https://english.belastingdienst-cn.nl/organisation/douane-caribisch-nederland) ★★★★★

> Leisure and sport vessels are **exempt from cargo import/export clearance** (no goods declaration if not carrying commercial cargo), but **the obligation to report to Customs and Immigration for people and vessel** and to obtain a departure permit remains. [Belastingdienst CN — Export clearance: leisure vessels exempt](https://english.belastingdienst-cn.nl/topics/customs-business/import-transit-and-export/export-clearance) ★★★★★

## Before Arrival: SailClear and Papers

1. **SailClear pre-notification expected by Customs (since 2023)** — file the notification at [sailclear.com](https://www.sailclear.com) **before you set sail**: on the quay at Kralendijk **there is no computer available** to complete it. It shortens time in the office but **does not replace appearing in person**. Cruising sources aggregate ★★★; [SailClear FAQ](https://www.sailclear.com/faq) ★★★
2. Prepare a folder (originals + 2 copies):
   - passports for all crew (valid for the entire stay);
   - **vessel registration/nationality**, proof of ownership/insurance;
   - **crew list / passenger manifest** (full name, date of birth, nationality, passport number);
   - **outbound document (zarpe/despacho) from the previous port — mandatory**: without proof of the country of departure entry may be refused (check also enforced on arrivals from Venezuela/Los Roques). Cruising sources aggregate ★★★; [Doyle Guides — Bonaire Updates 30/05/2017](https://doyleguides.com/bonaire-updated-guide/) ★★
   - receipts for **Visitor Entry Tax** and **STINAPA Nature Fee** if already paid online (recommended within 48 h before arrival for the Entry Tax).
3. **Notify Harbour Village Marina about your mooring**: VHF **17** (mooring management) or **12** for availability before entry per secondary sources; tel +599 717 7419. Overnight moorings cannot be reserved, allocation is first-come first-served. [InfoBonaire — Yachting & Cruising](https://infobonaire.com/getting-to-bonaire/yachting-cruising/) ★★★; [NoForeignLand — Kralendijk](https://www.noforeignland.com/map/place/5730169490767872) ★★★

## On Arrival at Kralendijk — Quayside Procedure

**Only port of entry: Kralendijk — 12°09.1′ N 068°16.7′ W** (off Fort Oranje). [Ocean Posse — Bonaire](https://oceanposse.com/bonaire/) ★★★

1. **Never anchor**: the entire island perimeter down to the drop-off is **Bonaire National Marine Park** — anchoring is prohibited (exception only for traditional fishing boats using stones). Use **only the two-tone overnight moorings (white/red, rugby-ball shape with yellow pendant)** between 12°09.44′ N 068°17.09′ W (south of Harbour Village entrance) and 12°09.06′ N 068°16.41′ W (off Karel's Bar). [STINAPA — Mooring](https://stinapabonaire.org/rules-and-regulations/mooring/) ★★★★★; [STINAPA — Boating PDF](https://stinapabonaire.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/Boating.pdf) ★★★★★
2. Once secured, go by dinghy to the **dinghy dock at Karel's Bar** (bar/restaurant, free berthing with a drink) or directly to the Customs/Immigration jetty (200 m south of the dinghy dock, berths reserved for official craft — keep clear). [CruisersWiki — Bonaire](https://www.cruiserswiki.org/wiki/Bonaire) ★★
3. Report **immediately and in person — all hands** (Immigration headcount) at the **Customs + Immigration office in the same building at Fort Oranje / Plasa Wilhelmina 3** — Immigration desk hours Mon–Thu 08:00–11:30 and 13:30–16:00, Fri 08:00–11:30; Harbour Customs tel +599 715 8591. Fill in entry forms, show zarpe and vessel papers, declare weapons. [RijksdienstCN — Contact IND](https://english.rijksdienstcn.com/immigration-naturalisation/contact) ★★★★★; [Belastingdienst CN — Contact / Customs Bonaire Kaya L.D. Gerharts 12, tel +599 715 8585, harbour Plasa Wilhelmina 3 tel +599 715 8591](https://www.belastingdienst-cn.nl/service/contact) ★★★★★
4. **Weapons and similar**: pistols, rifles, **flare guns, spear guns** are treated as weapons: they must be **declared and deposited with Customs** for the duration of your stay, collected on departure. [InfoBonaire — Yachting & Cruising](https://infobonaire.com/getting-to-bonaire/yachting-cruising/) ★★★; [CruisersWiki — Bonaire](https://www.cruiserswiki.org/wiki/Bonaire) ★★; [Doyle Guides 2017](https://doyleguides.com/bonaire-updated-guide/) ★★
5. Pay on the spot if not already done online: **STINAPA Nature Fee US$ 40** (calendar year) and **Visitor Entry Tax US$ 75/10** (see 00). **Customs and Immigration charge no clearance fee** during normal hours. [Annual Report STINAPA 2022](https://stinapabonaire.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/STINAPA-Annual-Report-2022_Digital.pdf) ★★★★; [tourismtax.bonairegov.com](https://tourismtax.bonairegov.com) ★★★★★
6. STINAPA/mooring formalities: registration at the **Customs Office and Harbour Village Marina**, mooring assignment, briefing on mooring use (use your own line ≥6 m, do not tie off directly, max 18 m / ~55–58 ft, one boat per mooring, no commercial activity on board, respect visa duration). [STINAPA — Mooring](https://stinapabonaire.org/rules-and-regulations/mooring/) ★★★★★

### Handy Tip (Cruiser Experience)

If you arrive outside office hours (e.g. Sunday), take a free overnight mooring and report **first thing the next morning when the office opens** — do not remain unannounced via VHF/marina. If you temporarily leave your mooring for a daytime dive (max 2 h on the yellow day-use moorings), leave a fender/pendant on your mooring to signal occupation. [NoForeignLand — Kralendijk](https://www.noforeignland.com/map/place/5730169490767872) ★★★; [STINAPA — Boating PDF](https://stinapabonaire.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/Boating.pdf) ★★★★★

## Departure (Outbound Clearance)

- Return **to the same Customs/Immigration office** before slipping your mooring, with passports and vessel papers; request **zarpe/departure declaration and a duplicate departure card if needed** where the next country requires it. Collect any deposited weapons. [Belastingdienst CN — Export clearance: duplicate departure card on request](https://english.belastingdienst-cn.nl/topics/customs-business/import-transit-and-export/export-clearance) ★★★★★
- Advise Harbour Village Marina when you vacate the mooring; pump-out and waste disposal are **included** in the mooring fee — rangers may require a pump-out based on holding-tank capacity, people on board and length of stay. [NoForeignLand — Kralendijk](https://www.noforeignland.com/map/place/5730169490767872) ★★★

**Belastingdienst Caribisch Nederland — Douane Caribisch Nederland**, Kaya L.D. Gerharts 12, Postbus 329, Kralendijk · +599 715 8585 · [english.belastingdienst-cn.nl](https://english.belastingdienst-cn.nl/organisation/douane-caribisch-nederland) ★★★★★

## Moorings and Anchoring Ban (Impact on Clearance)

The Bonaire National Marine Park surrounds the entire island: **anchoring is prohibited on coral/protected bottom**. The only legal overnight stops for visitors are the **STINAPA overnight moorings** or a marina berth. Private moorings (white/yellow/orange) are reserved for residents with a STINAPA permit (waiting list ~3 years) — do not use them. Public yellow/blue day moorings are day-use only max 2 h, one boat >4 m per mooring (or up to 3 tenders <4 m). [STINAPA — Mooring](https://stinapabonaire.org/rules-and-regulations/mooring/) ★★★★★

## Cruisers' Experiences

- Entry described as “easy and friendly, free of charge, efficient; everyone must appear for headcount” — Customs holds flare/spear guns without hassle and returns them on departure. ★★ [CruisersWiki — Bonaire; Doyle Guides 2017]
- Harbour Village very responsive on VHF for mooring allocation; in high season moorings fill up — call before entry and have a long line ready (≥6 m). ★★★ [NoForeignLand — Kralendijk; InfoBonaire]
- Since 2023 Customs expect a pre-filed SailClear: those arriving without it find only paper forms and longer processing. ★★★ [Cruising sources aggregate ★★★
- **Failure to report / missing zarpe / undeclared weapons**: entry refused, precautionary seizure of the weapon, possible detention of the vessel pending regularisation, import duties if temporary admission is breached (commercial use, exceeding 6 months without extension). [Belastingdienst CN — permanent supervision](https://english.belastingdienst-cn.nl/organisation/douane-caribisch-nederland) ★★★★★; Curaçao practice 24h/6 months ★★★
- **Overstay (people)**: fine, removal and re-entry ban (KMar/IND); the period granted may already have been shortened at the border for insufficient means. [RijksdienstCN — Travel with non-Dutch passport](https://english.rijksdienstcn.com/justice--security/royal-netherlands-marechaussee/travel-with-a-non-dutch-passport) ★★★★★
- Pollution / black-water discharge without holding tank: working holding tank required to use the moorings; otherwise you must berth in a marina or leave Bonaire. [NoForeignLand — Kralendijk] ★★★

## To Verify Before Your Cruise

- Availability of overnight moorings in high season (Dec–Apr) and updated marina rates; contact Harbour Village before the passage.
- Updated WHO yellow-fever risk list if coming from South America/Africa.
- Any updates to SailClear/BES platform or VHF channel (17 vs 12) and IND/Customs hours — check [english.rijksdienstcn.com](https://english.rijksdienstcn.com/immigration-naturalisation/tourist-stay) and [belastingdienst-cn.nl](https://www.belastingdienst-cn.nl) before departure.
