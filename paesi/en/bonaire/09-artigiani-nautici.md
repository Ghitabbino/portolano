# 09 — Marine Trades & Chandlers

**Last checked: 28/08/2026**

The local hub is **small and scattered around Kralendijk**: for **major refits, haul-out and composite work** many owners rely on **regional hubs (Curaçao — Caracasbaai / Curaçao Marine, Aruba — Oranjestad, St. Martin/Antigua)**. For day-to-day needs (outboards, electrics, antifouling, hardware) Kralendijk offers 3–4 solid addresses a few minutes' walk from the moorings. Contact is typically by **phone/WhatsApp**; languages spoken are **Dutch, English, Papiamento and Spanish** (Swedish/German at Budget Marine). For major jobs always ask for a **written quote** and confirm lead times in high season (Dec–Apr); spares = 1–3 weeks if ordered via Miami/Europe.

- **Outboard/inboard engines**: **Boto Blanku (Yamaha)** + **Bonaire Marine Center (Suzuki/Simrad)** + **ABC Marine** → see table above and [04 Services](04-servizi-cantieri.md). Diagnostics also via Harbour Village.
- **On-board refrigeration/air conditioning**: **DATA MISSING** dedicated technician on Bonaire — ask Budget Marine or Harbour Village for a freelancer; structured support in Curaçao.
- **Electronics/power (lithium batteries, panels, inverters)**: **Bonaire Marine Center (Simrad/Navico, Uflex)** + Budget Marine (wiring, corrosion protection) → limited availability, better to order in advance via Miami/Netherlands.
- **Sails**: **no resident sailmaker** — urgent repairs with itinerant sailmaker in season; serious work at **Le Marin (Martinique) or Curacao Marine** ★★
- **Rigging**: mobile professionals from Curaçao/Aruba; check references before work aloft.
- **Antifouling/careening**: **Seajet via Bonaire Marine Center**, paints/antifouling from **Budget Marine / Carene Shop equivalent**, light careening at **Ocean Breeze / Bonaire Nautico / El Navegante**; 35 t travelift / 700 t dock at **Marina Bas-du-Fort (Guadeloupe) or Curaçao** for larger boats.
- **Gas/cylinders**: propane refill in the marina (Harbour Village US$4.50/lb) ★★★★ — check local adapters.

## Practical Notes

- **Payments**: cash USD (official currency), cards widely accepted; bank transfer for special orders via Budget Marine Group. Always ask for an **invoice** (useful for customs/insurance).
- **Lead times**: spares from Europe/USA = 1–3 weeks (DHL via Miami/Amsterdam); shops often order from distributors in **St. Maarten (Budget Marine Cole Bay)** or **Curaçao**.
- **On-board power**: lithium/Victron/Mastervolt — limited availability, order ahead for delivery **care of the marina stating boat name and mooring position**.
- **Antifouling**: available from Budget Marine (Seajet International) and Bonaire Marine Center; application by ABC Marine or El Navegante yard.
- **Hours**: almost everything closed on Sunday; Budget Marine closes at 12:00 on Saturday — plan purchases for Friday.
- **For Amel and French-built boats**: the dealers above work on all brands; for specific spares (e.g. Volvo/Yanmar) ask Boto Blanku/BMC and, if needed, order via Budget Marine St. Maarten or Curaçao.
