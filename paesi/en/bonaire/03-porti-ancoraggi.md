# 03 — Ports & Marinas

**Last checked: 28/08/2026** · Coordinates in decimal degrees / N-W as per source · datum WGS84

Bonaire is entirely surrounded by the **Bonaire National Marine Park**: **anchoring is prohibited** everywhere (coral bottom) except for traditional fishing boats <4 m using a coral stone. Legal stops are only on **STINAPA moorings** or alongside in a marina. All clearance takes place at **Kralendijk** (the only port of entry). [STINAPA — Mooring](https://stinapabonaire.org/rules-and-regulations/mooring/) ★★★★★; [STINAPA Boating PDF](https://stinapabonaire.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/Boating.pdf) ★★★★★; [InfoBonaire — Yachting & Cruising](https://infobonaire.com/getting-to-bonaire/yachting-cruising/) ★★★

## Leeward (West) Coast — The Only Workable, Sheltered Side

The entire west coast from Harbour Village to south of Kralendijk lies in the lee of the trades, with flat water and depth that drops away quickly. Here you find the two full-service marinas and the **30–51 two-tone overnight moorings (white/red, rugby-ball shape with yellow pendant)** for visitors between 12°09.44′ N 068°17.09′ W (south of Harbour Village entrance) and 12°09.06′ N 068°16.41′ W (off Karel's Bar). [STINAPA](https://stinapabonaire.org/rules-and-regulations/mooring/) ★★★★★

### Kralendijk — Harbour Village Marina ⚓ sole port of entry
- **12°09.83′ N 068°17.08′ W** · VHF **17** (town mooring allocation) and **12** (marina office) · VHF 16 listening watch · [harbourvillage.com/marina](https://www.harbourvillage.com/bonaire-marina) ★★★★
- **Only BES port of entry**: Customs/Immigration at **Plasa Wilhelmina 3 / Fort Oranje** (200 m south of Karel's Bar dinghy dock); SailClear pre-notification expected before departure, all crew must report in person (headcount). See [01 Clearance](01-clearance.md). [Belastingdienst CN](https://www.belastingdienst-cn.nl/service/contact) ★★★★★
- Full-service marina: **60 berths** for yachts up to **60 ft** + a mega-yacht pier for vessels up to **240 ft (Ambar Point)**; 30/50 A and 50 Hz, water, diesel/petrol, bunker >10,000 l via Curoil, pump-out, WiFi, showers, laundry, parking, security. [Harbour Village Marina](https://www.harbourvillage.com/bonaire-marina) ★★★★
- Management of **STINAPA overnight moorings for visitors**: allocation first-come first-served via Harbour Village Marina, payment on site; use **your own line ≥6 m** (do not tie directly to the buoy), one boat per mooring, max **18 m (55–58 ft)**, holding tank mandatory, no commercial activity on board, stay limited to visa duration. [STINAPA](https://stinapabonaire.org/rules-and-regulations/mooring/) ★★★★★
- Dinghy dock: take the tender to **Karel's Bar** (a drink appreciated) or the Customs/Immigration jetty (berths reserved for official craft — keep clear). Always lock dinghy and outboard. ★★
- Contact: tel **+599 717 7419** / WhatsApp **+599 701 7500** · dockmaster@harbourvillage.com · Office hours: Mon–Fri 08:30–12:00 & 13:30–17:00, Sat 08:00–13:00. [Harbour Village Marina](https://www.harbourvillage.com/bonaire-marina) ★★★★
- Shelter: good protection from the trades; exposed to north/west swell — check the local forecast before entering, especially with winter cold fronts. ★★

### Plaza Marina (Van der Valk Plaza Beach & Dive Resort)
- **J.A. Abraham Blvd 80, Kralendijk (~12°08.6′ N 068°16.5′ W — DATA MISSING verified official coordinate)** · tel **+599 717 2500** / marina@bonaire.valk.com · VHF DATA MISSING ★★★
- Resort marina a few minutes' walk from central Kralendijk; floating berths for leisure and charter boats (Bonaire Yacht Charters), with resort access (pools, PADI 5★ dive centre). Ideal if you are staying at the resort or for a short stop with hotel facilities. [Plaza Resort — Marina](https://www.plazaresortbonaire.com/en/facilities/marina) ★★★
- Berths managed via **Plaza Boat Club**: 24/7 surveillance, gated access, haul-out and hurricane protection, fuel/water/power alongside. Rates see below. [Plaza Boat Club](https://plazaboatclub.com/) ★★★

### Klein Bonaire
- Uninhabited island **0.8 km (0.4 NM)** to leeward of Kralendijk, a nature reserve and turtle nesting ground. Only **yellow/blue day-use moorings max 2 h** (one boat >4 m per mooring or up to 3 tenders <4 m), **overnight stay prohibited**; max **13 m**. Respect the reef and underwater trails. [STINAPA](https://stinapabonaire.org/rules-and-regulations/mooring/) ★★★★★

### Other West-Coast Moorings (Day-Use)
- Dozens of public yellow/blue moorings along the dive coast (Buddy Dive, Harbour Village, Eden Beach etc.): **max 2 h**, first-come first-served, not for overnight stays. Private moorings (white/yellow/orange) are reserved for residents with a STINAPA permit (waiting list ~3 years) — **not available to visitors**. [STINAPA](https://stinapabonaire.org/rules-and-regulations/mooring/) ★★★★★

## Windward (East) Coast, Lac Bay and Washington Slagbaai — Not Tenable Overnight
- The windward east coast is exposed to the Atlantic, with continuous reef, surf and no shelter: **no harbour, no moorings, no recommended anchorage** for cruising yachts. Lac Bay (south-east lagoon, RAMSAR/mangroves) is a reserve and kiteboarding area with very shallow water — unsuitable for sailing yachts. Washington Slagbaai (north-west) is a land park with no nautical facilities. Approach only in settled weather with local support, never for an overnight stay. ★★
