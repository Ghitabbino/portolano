# 07 — Provisioning

**Last checked: 28/08/2026**

## General Price Level — Bonaire

- Groceries are more expensive than in Europe/NL due to imports; still average for the southern Caribbean. USD has been the legal currency since 2011. ★★★
- Good value local produce: fresh fish (Daily Catch), tropical fruit & veg, Dutch-style bread/bakery (Van den Tweel bakery). ★★★

### Central Kralendijk / Boulevard

Top Supermarket and mini-markets (Liangxiang, Wing Cheung, Caribe Nobo) are **walkable from the dinghy dock/boulevard** (town centre a few hundred metres away). Limited range: for a big shop go to Kaya Industria. ★★

### Outside Kralendijk

Rincon, Nikiboko, Antriol, Belnem: neighbourhood toko/mini-market, basic range; for fresh produce/variety return to Kralendijk. Opening hours vary — **DATA MISSING** verified details for each district at next check. ★★

## Markets

- **Daily Catch Fisherman's Market**: Kaya L.D. Gerharts, Kralendijk — fresh catch daily from local fishermen. ★★★
- Mobile produce market and roadside stalls: local fruit/veg early morning (Kralendijk, Rincon); where absent, itinerant fruit trucks. ★★
- No large covered market like Fort-de-France; spices/tropical fruit in supermarkets or at stalls. ★★

## Practical Tips

- Folding trolley + cool bags: essential; everything from Van den Tweel/Warehouse is a short taxi ride away (flat fare per destination, agree before boarding) or walkable.
- Bottled water: stock up if leaving the moorings for Klein Bonaire/Lac; ice available in supermarkets — check on public holidays.
- Currency **USD** everywhere; Van den Tweel/Warehouse take cards, BonDiGro cash/local card only — bring cash.
