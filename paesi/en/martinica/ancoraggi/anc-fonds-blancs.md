# Fonds Blancs {#anc-fonds-blancs}

[← All Anchorages](../08-ancoraggi.md)
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Location** |White-sand banks among the îlets (Madame, Oscar…) off Le François |
| **Depth** |1–3 m over the banks — milky water over white sand |
| **Holding** |Sand; take care not to dry out in very shallow water |
| **Shelter** |Partly sheltered by the îlets; trades still felt |
| **Hazards** |**Tricky bathymetry**: approaches between the banks only with high sun and close attention to the chart; tour-boat traffic |
| **Crowding** |Very crowded on holidays and weekends (picnic dinghies); quieter mid-week |
| **Restrictions** |Baie du François is among the regulated areas >50 m; respect ZMEL buoys and sailing-club reserved channels; check Le François municipal buoyage plan ★★★★|
| **Note** |Built from pilot-book consensus → depths/passes **to be verified on the local chart** |

- SHOM charts show regulated areas and channels; available from authorised dealers (Picksea, Eau Bleue, NV Charts PoD). ★★★★
- **Free administrative charting**: interactive maps from the Direction de la Mer + ODE “Mémento réglementaire de la mer” (regulatory handbook) PDF with all ZMEL/closed zones. ★★★★
- For everyday navigation: official SHOM/Navionics electronic chart on the plotter + the paper charts above as mandatory backup.

## Not Covered (for now)

- **Baie des Anglais** (south, Cap Salomon): often quoted as wild and beautiful → insufficient data, **to be verified**
- **Anse Couleuvre / Le Prêcheur** (north): exposed, only in settled weather ★★★
- **Baie du Vauclin / Robert / Trinité** (Atlantic coast): attractive but exposed to the trades and full of fish pots → see [06 Safety](../06-sicurezza.md)

## Martinique Anchor Checklist

1. Official mouillage chart downloaded (Mémento ODE) ✓
2. Main anchor sized for sand/mud (excellent holding almost everywhere)
3. Long boat hook for French rigid mooring rings
4. Watch for nearly-invisible transparent fish-pot buoys all along the west–south coast
5. Navily app installed if heading for Saint-Pierre
