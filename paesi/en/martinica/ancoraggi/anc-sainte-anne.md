# Sainte-Anne — the classic {#anc-sainte-anne}

[← All Anchorages](../08-ancoraggi.md)
**14°26.3′N 60°53.1′W** ★★

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Depth** |~5 m off the village, gentle shelving over sand; room for hundreds of boats ★★|
| **Holding** |Excellent holding on sand ★★★|
| **Shelter** |Good shelter from E/ENE trades, limited swell; “Sainte-Anne Nord” variant even more swell-free ★★★|
| **Hazards** |Dinghy dock: hull can slip underneath in a chop (anchor astern or lift the bow); fishing lines reported; dinghy theft possible ★★|
| **Crowding** | High in season (Dec–Apr) — arrive early; local weekends busy |
| **Restrictions** |⛔ Réserve naturelle des îlets: anchoring forbidden within **300 m of Îlets Hardy, Percé, Burgaux and Poirier**, land only at site moorings ★★★★; regulated zone >50 m ★★★★; swim zones off the beach ★★|
| **Ashore** | Village with shops/restaurants a few minutes’ walk from the dinghy dock; clearance at Snack BOUBOU |
