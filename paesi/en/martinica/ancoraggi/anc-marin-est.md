# Le Marin Est/Sud {#anc-marin-est}

[← All Anchorages](../08-ancoraggi.md)
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Depth** |Approx. 3–8 m in the cul-de-sac |
| **Holding** |Sand, “perfect” according to Navily ★★★|
| **Shelter** |Very well sheltered from wind and swell (enclosed bay, hills behind) ★★★|
| **Hazards** |Wrecks/abandoned hulls in places — watch soundings around them ★★; dinghy traffic to the marina|
| **Crowding** |Large roadstead, accommodates many boats even in high season ★★★|
| **Restrictions** |⛔ Trous à cyclone: anchoring forbidden in the 3 cyclone-hole zones in the cul-de-sac (AOT only) ★★★★; ⛔ careening area closed north of the Trou Manuel line → use the fishing-harbour east quay ★★★★; ⛔ Sainte-Luce: south of Pointe Borgnèse anchoring forbidden everywhere, buoys only on the zone edge ★★★★; bay regulated >50 m ★★★★|
| **Highlights** | Ideal base for services/haul-out; Morne Gommier trail for the panorama |
