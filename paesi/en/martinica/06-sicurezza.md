## General Picture

- Canadian government advisory (updated 29/07/2026 ★★★★): **"Take normal security precautions"** (*Prenez des mesures de sécurité normales*) — lowest alert level. Petty crime (pickpocketing, snatch theft, car break-ins) concentrated in Fort-de-France; avoid deserted beaches and isolated spots after dark.
- Préfecture annual report 2025 (presented 04/02/2026 ★★★★★): property crime (*atteintes aux biens*) **−6.3%**, burglaries −10%, vehicle theft −9%; overall rate **20.51 incidents/1,000 inhabitants vs 27.71 French average** — exception: violent theft (*vols avec violence*) above average; assault +5.1%.
- Drug-related homicides: 29 by firearm in 2024, 17 in H1 2025; attempted murders 88 (2023) → 199 (2024). Victims almost exclusively within trafficking circles, concentrated in Fort-de-France ★★★.
- Local press 2025–2026: armed robberies in Fort-de-France (Terres-Sainville, Dillon, rue Lamartine, city centre with RAID intervention Sep 2025), restaurant at Sainte-Luce (Nov 2025), gunfire on the beach at Anse Moustique and Anse Meurnier during *after yoles* Aug 2026 (young local victims) ★★★.
- No restrictive French advisory; tourist island with European-standard infrastructure.

## Theft Targeting Cruisers

Data **Caribbean Safety and Security Net** (the.org ★★★), checked 21/08/2026:

- **37 incidents in the last 5 years within 10 km of Le Marin**: almost all dinghy and outboard theft, some burglaries aboard at anchor, vandalism. No violence against persons.
- Recurring hotspots: Leader Price / Carrefour dinghy dock (2022×2, 2023, 2024), Indigo dock (2019, Jun 2026), marina (Apr 2022, Oct 2024, Aug 2025 ×2), Le Marin anchorage (burglary Jun/Sep 2022, Sep 2025, May 2026), Carenantilles yard (Nov 2024), Sainte-Anne (series 2022–2026), Baie des Anglais (Jul 2021).
- Even **chained** dinghies have been taken (Sep 2022, Aug 2024).
- Effective countermeasures reported by cruisers: short chain + padlock through the transom, engine always locked to the tender with a dedicated outboard lock, tender hoisted aboard at night, alarm on the dinghy, nothing left on view either in the dinghy or on deck.
- Reporting incidents: (online report), *gendarmerie maritime*, CROSS. FB group "Martinique Cruisers" for rapid alerts ★★.
