# 09 — Marine Artisans & Chandlers

The undisputed hub is the **port of Le Marin**: mechanics, riggers, sailmakers, refrigeration engineers and electricians are clustered between the marina, Bd Allègre and the Carenantilles yard (Usine du Marin). Contact is typically by phone or WhatsApp; French is spoken and almost always English. For major jobs always ask for a **devis** (written quote) and confirm lead times in high season (Dec–Apr), when workshops are fully booked.

## Practical Notes

- **Payment**: many trades accept French bank transfer; for small jobs cash/card. Always ask for an invoice (useful for customs/insurance).
- **Lead times**: spares from Europe = 1–3 weeks (DHL/Colissimo); workshops often order from metropolitan distributors. Common cruiser practice: order online (Victron, lithium, AMEL spares etc.) for delivery **to Marina du Marin marked with boat name and berth number**.
- **Onboard power**: electrical components (battery chargers, inverters, solar controllers) available from **Caraïbe Énergie** (Caraïbe Marine division); complex installations also handled by Martinique Nautic and YES.
- **Antifouling**: available from yard shops (Carene Shop) and chandlers; haul-out at Carenantilles or the yard areas in Le Marin.
- **For AMEL yachts**: the firms above work on all brands; for the original aluminium mast rigging and AMEL-specific spares contact Caraïbe Marine (rigger) and Mécanique Plaisance (Yanmar/Volvo engine on AMEL yachts) first.
