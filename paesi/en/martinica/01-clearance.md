# 01 — Yacht Clearance

**Last checked: 21/08/2026** · Main source: martinique.gouv.fr (updated 30/01/2026) ★★★★★

## Who Must Clear In / Out

All pleasure craft — any nationality and flag — that:
- **enter** French Antillean waters (Martinique, Guadeloupe, St-Barth, St-Martin) from a foreign port/territorial waters or from offshore;
- **depart** for a foreign port/waters or offshore;
- **move between the French islands** (mandatory even for Martinique↔Guadeloupe etc. since 01/09/2024). [martinique.gouv.fr 30/01/2026 ★★★★★]

## Online Procedure (since 01/09/2024)

1. Create an **account** on the portal and keep it — your documents remain saved and reusable.
   - Current URL: `https://demarche.numerique.gouv.fr/commencer/declaration-clearance-antilles` [martinique.gouv.fr 30/01/2026 ★★★★★]
   - Legacy URL still working: `https://www.demarches-simplifiees.fr/commencer/declaration-clearance-antilles` ★★★★★
2. Complete the declaration with: vessel details (name, registration, flag, MMSI, type, length, tonnage…), owner, **crew/passenger list** (full name, date of birth, nationality, passport/ID no.), declarable stores, previous and next country, date/time/place of arrival and departure, intended length of stay, skipper's signature.
3. Submit: the *attestation* arrives by email and is saved to your account.

### Useful Tip (Feb 2025 ★★★)

On the platform you can **duplicate a previous declaration** and change only dates/ports — for departure, duplicate the inbound declaration and just update the date. Saves re-typing everything.

## Stamped Hard Copy

- Until the other Caribbean states recognise the electronic declaration, **print the attestation**: you need the paper copy to clear in at NON-French countries (e.g. Saint Lucia). [martinique.gouv.fr ★★★★★; Feb 2025 ★★★]
- With the online form alone you **do not have to call at an official port**, unless you need the stamp. ★★★
- The stamp is obtained at an **Approved Clearance Point** (*Point agréé Clearance*, official list v15/12/2025): take the file on your phone/tablet or log in on their computer; they print and stamp it. Cost **€5**. ★★★

## What Cruisers Report

- Le Marin capitainerie: quick procedure, short queues early morning. ★★
- CAP Ponton: handy to do clearance + fuel in one stop; crowded on Saturdays. ★★

## Penalties

Failure to declare is punishable under Art. 410 *code des douanes*, Art. 43 Ord. 2016-1687, Art. L.5242-2 *code des transports*. The attestation must be kept aboard and produced on demand to any French or foreign authority. ★★★★★
