# 05 — Seasonality & Weather

**Last checked: 21/08/2026**

## Climate

- Average temperature **26 °C year-round**, maritime climate with **moderate trades** and heavy showers. ★★★

## Seasons

| Period | Characteristics |
|---|---|
| **December – April/May** | Dry, cooler season with steady trades: **best time to cruise** |
| **July – November** | **Hurricane season**: many insurers impose area restrictions; Le Marin has a *trou à cyclone* (hurricane hole) but no shelter is guaranteed |

★★★

## Hurricane Instructions (official ★★★★)

Pontoon and mooring construction standards are compliant, BUT the harbour cannot be held liable for damage from failure of moorings/ground tackle caused by a cyclone. Document: *Consignes aux usagers en cas de cyclones* (Instructions to users in the event of a cyclone) (marin.marina-martinique.fr).

## Hurricanes

Season **June–November**, peak August–October. Martinique has been struck by major cyclones in 1891, 1963 (Edith), 1979 (David), 2007 (Dean Cat. 2). Hurricane plan: préfecture *consignes* and harbour offices publish securing procedures; Marina du Marin requires contracts in advance for winter storage. [Météo-France ★★★★★; préfecture ★★★★★]

## Local Winds

- ENE trades typical of the Windwards; gusty katabatics off the high ground in west-coast bays. ★★
- Inter-island passages (Saint Lucia Channel): beware collisions with cetaceans — a catamaran holed by a whale in the channel in 2017, the third such case in a few months according to the harbourmaster. ★★

## Typical Weather Windows

Passages Martinique→Saint Lucia (22 M S) or →Dominica (30 M NW): feasible with trades ≤20 kt, best in the early morning when the wind is lightest. The Saint Lucia Channel (110 M towards Guadeloupe) is best with a post-frontal window in the dry season. ★★★★
