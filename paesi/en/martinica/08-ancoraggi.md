# 08 — Pilot of Anchorages

**Last checked: 21/08/2026**

## General Anchoring Rules (Read First)

- **Yachts up to 50 m**: free anchoring allowed anywhere, EXCEPT the prohibited areas below and except over **protected coral** (arrêté of 25/06/2017): anchor and chain must not rest on reef. ★★★★
- **Yachts over 50 m**: only at the exact positions in arrêté R02-2018-11-22-002 in 8 regulated bays: Saint-Pierre, Fort-de-France, Anses d'Arlet, Le Marin, Vauclin, François, Robert, Trinité. ★★★★

### ⛔ Absolute No-Anchoring Zones (Official)

| Area | Prohibition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Réserve naturelle des îlets de Sainte-Anne** | Anchoring prohibited within **300 m of every islet** (Hardy, Percé, Burgaux, Poirier); moor only on site buoys | Arrêté 09-00872 ★★★★|
| **Wrecks off Saint-Pierre** | Anchoring prohibited over the historic wrecks off the town | Observatoire ★★★★|
| **Trous à cyclone in Cul-de-Sac du Marin** (3 zones) | Anchoring prohibited, temporary and permanent, except with an AOT (public maritime domain authorisation) | Arrêté préfectoral ★★★★|
| **Careening area at Le Marin** | Prohibited north of the line mouth of Ravine Trou Manuel → eastern quay of the fishing harbour | Arrêté 27/07/2016 ★★★★|
| **Marine protected area at Sainte-Luce** (from south of Pointe Borgnèse to Pointe Philippeaux) | Anchoring prohibited everywhere and at all times; moor only on buoys at the edge of the zone | Arrêté 18/02/2000 ★★★★|
| **Baie du Trésor** (Caravelle) | Anchoring prohibited throughout the bay except by day in the northern part of the beach bight; personal watercraft prohibited | Arrêté ★★★★|
| **Baie des Flamands – Fort-de-France** | Prohibited: access/swinging area for cruise terminal, Pointe Simon ferry channel, strip along Fort Saint-Louis (between the shore and points D–E) | Arrêté 26/10/2006 ★★★★|
| **RNRM du Prêcheur (Albert Falco reserve)** | Anchoring only in APID harbour areas and in existing professional sectors; prohibited elsewhere (except emergency) | Délibération CR972 ★★★★|

Full texts and official charts: page *"Les zones d'interdiction de mouillage"* (No-anchoring zones) on the [Observatoire de l'eau](https://www.observatoire-eau-martinique.fr/mer-et-littoral/usages-et-pressions-2/mouillage/les-zones-dinterdiction-de-mouillage/) (downloadable PDFs).

- There are also **ZMEL** (organised mooring fields) and **communal** buoyage plans: charting is on the Observatoire site (*Mémento réglementaire de la mer* — Regulatory Handbook of the Sea) and on the commune-by-commune pages of the [Direction de la Mer](https://www.dm.martinique.developpement-durable.gouv.fr/mouillage-navigation-et-loisirs-nautiques-r183.html). ★★★★
- Buoyage is **IALA-B — reversed** compared with mainland Europe. ★★★

- SHOM charts show regulated areas and channels; available from authorised dealers (Picksea, Eau Bleue, NV Charts PoD). ★★★★
- **Free administrative charting**: interactive maps from the Direction de la Mer + *Mémento réglementaire de la mer* (Regulatory Handbook of the Sea) PDF (ODE) covering all ZMEL / prohibited zones. ★★★★
- For daily navigation: official SHOM / Navionics electronic chart on the plotter + the paper charts above as mandatory backup.

## Not Included (For Now)

- **Baie des Anglais** (south, Cap Salomon): often cited as wild and beautiful → insufficient data, **to be verified**
- **Anse Couleuvre / Le Prêcheur** (north): exposed, only in settled weather ★★★
- **Baie du Vauclin / Robert / Trinité** (Atlantic coast): attractive but exposed to the trades and full of fish traps → see [06 Security](06-sicurezza.md)

## Anchor Checklist for Martinique

1. Official mooring chart downloaded (Mémento ODE) ✓
2. Main anchor suitable for sand/mud (holding excellent almost everywhere)
3. Long boathook for rigid French buoy rings
4. Watch for near-invisible fishing buoys along the ENTIRE west–south coast
5. Navily app installed if heading for Saint-Pierre
