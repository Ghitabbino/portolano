# 02 — Cost of Living

**Last updated: 24/08/2026**

Currency: **dollaro USA (USD, $)**. Sales tax combinata (IVU) **11,5%** su beni e servizi; i prezzi esposti spesso **non includono** l'imposta alla cassa. Livello prezzi alimentari poco sopra media USA continentale ma inferiore a molte isole caraibiche indipendenti. ★★★★★

## Cash & Payments

US Dollars, credit/debit cards accepted almost everywhere (Visa/Mastercard/Amex). ATMs widespread in cities and shopping malls; cash useful for *colmados*, markets and taxis outside San Juan. Expected tips: **15–18%** at the restaurant, **10–15%** taxi if good service. ATM in Fajardo, San Juan, Ponce.
