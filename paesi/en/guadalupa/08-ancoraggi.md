# 08 — Pilot of Anchorages

**Last updated: 21/08/2026 · positions approximate, navigate with official charts**

## General Anchoring Rules (First and Foremost)

- **Yachts up to 50 m**: free anchoring permitted everywhere EXCEPT the reserves below and regulated harbour areas.
- **IALA-B buoyage** — reversed compared with Europe (IALA-A). ★★★
- Coral bottoms in the National Park: anchor and chain must not rest on coral.

### ⛔ Reserves & Prohibitions

| Area | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Réserve Cousteau** (Îlets Pigeon) |Heart of the National Park: **anchoring prohibited**, mooring buoys only (for vessels <8 m); snorkelling/diving regulated ★★★|
| **Petite Terre** |National nature reserve: anchoring prohibited, **mooring buoys only** (9×10 t + 1×30 t), advance booking advised ★★★|
| Grand Cul-de-Sac Marin / Petit Cul-de-Sac | National Park: protection zones, anchoring regulated over coral |
| Harbours and channels | Mooring regulated in the Pointe-à-Pitre roadstead |

## Official Charts

SHOM Guadeloupe/Dominica series (see shom.fr for current editions) + Navionics/C-MAP. Buoyage **IALA-B** (reversed vs Europe). Watch the Mouchoir Carré shoals off Bas-du-Fort and the reef around Petite Terre.

## Not Included (For Now)

- **North Atlantic coast** (Anse-Bertrand, Port-Louis, Le Moule): anchor only in settled conditions, fish traps → see [06 Safety & Security](06-sicurezza.md).
- **Grand Cul-de-Sac Marin** (mangroves): quiet anchorages but local pilot needed for the passes.
- **Petite Terre east coast**: no shelter.

## Anchor Checklist for Guadeloupe

1. Clearance done? → [01](01-clearance.md)
2. NE trades forecast ≤20 kn on exposed passages?
3. NW swell coming? (rolls entire west coast)
4. Inside a reserve? → rules table above
5. Fish traps reported on the Atlantic route?
6. SHOM/Navionics chart up to date + IALA-B in mind
7. Petite Terre buoy booked?
