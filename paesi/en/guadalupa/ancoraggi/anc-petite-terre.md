# Petite Terre {#anc-petite-terre}

[← All anchorages](../08-ancoraggi.md)

Nature reserve: iguanas, turtles, dream seabeds. **Mooring buoys only** (9×10 t + 1×30 t), book in advance; entering via the pass with swell needs caution and high sun overhead. Spectacular snorkelling after the day-trippers leave. No services ashore: bring water and provisions. ★★★
