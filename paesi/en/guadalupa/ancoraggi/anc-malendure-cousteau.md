# Malendure — Îlets Pigeon (Cousteau Reserve) {#anc-malendure-cousteau}

[← All anchorages](../08-ancoraggi.md)
<div class="mapframe" data-slug="malendure-cousteau" data-lat="16.172" data-lon="-61.780" data-name="Malendure / Îlets Pigeon"></div>

The best snorkelling in the archipelago: Cousteau Reserve, turtles, coral. Anchorage on sand outside the reserve off Plage de Malendure (4–8 m, moderate holding); reserve moorings only for <8 m boats. Rolly with south-westerly. Ashore: dive centres, supermarket, laundry, petrol station 5 min on foot. ★★★
