# Ravine Paul Thomas (Basse-Terre) {#anc-ravine-paul-thomas}

[← All anchorages](../08-ancoraggi.md)
<div class="mapframe" data-slug="ravine-paul-thomas" data-lat="16.0080" data-lon="-61.7380" data-name="Ravine Paul Thomas"></div>

The highest-rated anchorage by cruisers (★4.9): excellent holding on sand, sheltered from the trades, snorkelling on both sides, tranquil atmosphere. Tidal currents at the entrance. Base for La Soufrière and the waterfalls. ⚠️ No services: nature only.
