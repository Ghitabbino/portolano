# Désirade — Baie Mahault {#anc-desireade}

[← All anchorages](../08-ancoraggi.md)
<div class="mapframe" data-slug="desireade" data-lat="16.3292839" data-lon="-61.0086499" data-name="Baie Mahault, La Désirade"></div>

The oldest geological island in the world: wild plateau, colourful villages, abandoned leper hospital. Baie Mahault is sheltered from the NE trades, sand/weed bottom in 4–7 m; rolls with southerly. Ferry from Saint-François. Small shops and restaurants.

## Official Charts

SHOM series Guadeloupe/Dominica (check shom.fr for updated editions) + Navionics/C-MAP. Buoyage **IALA-B** (reversed vs Europe). Beware the Mouchoir Carré shoals off Bas-du-Fort and the Petite Terre pass.

## Not included (for now)

- **North Atlantic coast** (Anse-Bertrand, Port-Louis, Le Moule): anchorage only in settled conditions, fishing pots → see [06 Safety & security](../06-sicurezza.md).
- **Grand Cul-de-Sac marin** (mangroves): quiet anchorages but local pilot needed for the passes.
- **Petite Terre east coast**: no shelter.

## Anchor Checklist for Guadeloupe

1. Clearance done? → [01 Clearance](../01-clearance.md)
2. NE trade ≤20 kn forecast on exposed passages?
3. NW swell coming? (rolls the entire west coast)
4. Inside a reserve? → table of rules above
5. Fishing pots reported on the Atlantic route?
6. SHOM/Navionics chart updated + IALA-B in mind
7. Petite Terre mooring booked?
