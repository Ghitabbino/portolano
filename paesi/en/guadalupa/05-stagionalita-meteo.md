# 05 — Seasonality & Weather

**Last updated: 21/08/2026**

## Climate

Hot and humid year-round: 24–30 °C, sea 26–29 °C. Dry season (*carême*) December–April; wet season (*hivernage*) July–November with heavy showers and increased cyclonic activity. [Météo-France ★★★★★]

## Seasons

| Period | Conditions |
|---|---|
| **Dec–Apr** | High season: NE trades 15–25 kn, variable sky, 26–29 °C, lower humidity |
| **May–Jun** | Transition: lighter and more unsettled winds, hot and humid, first tropical waves |
| **Jul–Nov** | Hurricane season (peak Aug–Sep): trades often light, sea >28 °C, real cyclonic risk |

## Cyclone Instructions (Préfecture ★★★★★)

Prefectoral hurricane plan: **nautical instructions (*consignes nautiques*)** are published daily during the cyclone season (alert levels 1–4, navigation bans and mandatory securing of yachts). Source: Préfecture de Guadeloupe + Météo-France.

## Hurricanes

Guadeloupe lies in the heart of the hurricane belt: **Maria (September 2017)** devastated the archipelago (notably Deshaies and Basse-Terre). A lay-up plan is mandatory: Bas-du-Fort floating dock, ashore at Caraïbes Docks or run south (Grenada). Insurers require a written plan by set dates (usually 1 June).

## Local Winds

- **NE trade wind** steady year-round, accelerated along the south coast of Grande-Terre (funnelling between Pointe des Châteaux and Marie-Galante).
- **Les Saintes Channel**: gusts between the islands and Sec Pâté; short, confused seas.
- **West Basse-Terre**: leeward, flat sea, but afternoon valley breeze.
- **South-westerly swell**: rare but makes every anchorage on the west coast roll (Deshaies, Malendure, Ravine Paul Thomas).

## Typical Windows

Crossings Guadeloupe→Dominica or →Antigua: 30–40 M, feasible with trades ≤20 kn; best early morning when wind is lightest. The Guadeloupe–Martinique channel (110 M S) is best sailed on a post-frontal window in the dry season.
