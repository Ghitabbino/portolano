# 00 — Entry, Documents & Visas

**Last checked: 21/08/2026 · official sources verified**

## Status

Guadeloupe is a **French overseas department (DROM)**: part of the EU as an outermost region but **outside the Schengen Area**, exactly like Martinique. Same regime for people; single EU customs territory for yachts.

## EU Citizens — No Visa Required

| Item | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Visa | **Not required** for EU citizens for overseas territories | immigration.interieur.gouv.fr, "Les visas pour l'outre-mer" ★★★★★ |
| Documents | **Valid national ID card or passport** — ID card suffices for Guadeloupe/Martinique | Service-Public F591, checked 10/12/2024 ★★★★★ |
| Stay ≤3 months | "You may enter and stay up to 3 months in France (including overseas) without special formalities" [orig.: *vous pouvez entrer et séjourner jusqu'à 3 mois en France, y compris outre-mer*] | Service-Public F10610, checked 20/11/2024 ★★★★★ |

⚠️ **Transit warning**: if the route transits a non-EU country (e.g. Sint Maarten, Antigua), a **passport is required**. [Service-Public F591 ★★★★★]

## Important Note for Arrival by Sea

- **People checks** (police) are separate from **yacht clearance** (customs): clearance is mandatory for all vessels to/from abroad → see [01 — Clearance](01-clearance.md).
- When arriving directly from a foreign island (Dominica, Antigua, Montserrat) have ID documents for all crew ready: the online declaration requires them one by one. [2024, martinique.gouv.fr ★★★★★]

## Beyond 3 Months? (EU Citizens)

**No formalities to complete**: there is no permit to renew nor extension to request.

| Situation | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Tourist stay beyond 3 months | For EU/Swiss citizens **there is no specific limit** on tourist stays; the *carte de séjour* in France is **optional** | Les Îles de Guadeloupe (official tourist board), FAQ 04/06/2025 ★★★★; Service-Public F22116 ★★★★★ |
| Schengen 90/180 quota | **Does not apply**: Schengen rules apply only to the European territory of France (Art. 138 Schengen Implementing Convention). Days spent here do NOT count toward the European quota; conversely a Schengen visa does not allow entry into the Antilles | immigration.interieur.gouv.fr ★★★★★; Service-Public F10610 ★★★★★ |
| Long stays | As everywhere in the EU (Directive 2004/38): sufficient means and health insurance; no document required unless you voluntarily apply for a title | Service-Public F10610 ★★★★★ |

In practice: **you may stay indefinitely** (as a tourist) with a valid ID card alone. For local work or permanent residence, tax and residency rules change — seek dedicated advice.

## The Yacht: No Time Limit

| Yacht | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **EU VAT-paid** (typical case: Italian-flagged yacht) | EU customs territory → **free movement, no time limit, no cruising permit**. Only online declarative clearance on arrival/departure from outside the EU | douane.gouv.fr ★★★★★ |
| Non-EU | **Temporary Admission (TA)**: max **18 months** within the EU customs territory (Arts. 250–253 UCC); renewable by exiting EU territory (call at a third-country port in the logbook = proof); 10-year cumulative cap. In the Antilles also valid within the Single Market of the Antilles/OECS | douane.gouv.fr "Régime particulier : l'admission temporaire", updated 15/02/2024 ★★★★★; EU Customs Code Art. 251 ★★★★★; bateaux-antilles.fr ★★★ |

⚠️ Non-EU yacht under TA: no charter and no sale within the EU, private use by non-EU residents only — violation triggers immediate taxation. ★★★★★

## Vaccinations

No mandatory vaccination from the EU. Mosquito protection recommended (dengue present). ★★★★
