# 01 — Yacht Customs Clearance

**Last checked: 21/08/2026**

## Who Must Clear

All pleasure craft, any nationality and flag, that:
- **enter** French Antillean waters coming from a foreign port / foreign territorial waters or the high seas;
- **depart** for a foreign port / foreign waters or the high seas;
- **move between the French islands of the Antilles** (mandatory also Guadeloupe ↔ Martinique etc. since 01/09/2024). [martinique.gouv.fr 30/01/2026 ★★★★★]

## Online Procedure (Mandatory Since 09/2024)

All the French Antilles use the same portal: **https://www.demarches-simplifiees.fr/commencer/declaration-clearance-antilles** — entry and departure declaration for vessels to/from abroad. [/ ★★★★; 2024, martinique.gouv.fr ★★★★★]

1. Complete the online form (vessel, crew, origin/destination) **before** arrival or immediately after.
2. Keep the PDF receipt sent by email: it is the document to show if inspected.
3. No stamp, no queue: *points agréés* are only needed if you want assistance or have no internet.

After the first clearance in a French Antillean port, movements between Martinique, Guadeloupe, Saint-Barth and Saint-Martin are **free and without formalities** (same customs territory). ★★★★★

## Stamped Paper Copy

- Until other Caribbean states recognise the electronic declaration, **print the *attestation***: a paper copy is required to check in to NON-French countries (e.g. Antigua, Dominica). [martinique.gouv.fr ★★★★★]
- The stamp (if required) is obtained at a **Point agréé Clearance** for **€5**; with the online form alone you are not obliged to enter an official port. ★★★

## Regional Customs

Direction Régionale des Douanes de Guadeloupe — 151 allée Maurice Micaux, 97102 Basse-Terre · +590 590 994 530 · dr-guadeloupe@douane.finances.gouv.fr ★★★★★

## Cruisers' Reports

- Les Saintes (ISM): simple check-in, 14:00–16:00; internet and laundry available on site. ★★★
- Deshaies: quick procedure but staff sometimes absent during advertised hours — avoid lunchtime. ★★★
- Pointe-à-Pitre (Bas-du-Fort): office crowded in the morning and at lunchtime; electronic clearance at the harbour office 08:00–17:30 (08:00–13:00 / 14:00–17:30 off-season), Sun/public holidays 08:00–12:00. ★★★

## Penalties

Failure to declare arrival/departure to/from outside the EU is a customs offence: financial penalties and possible seizure until regularised. With the online system there is no excuse: file before leaving the anchorage. [Code des douanes Art. 411 et seq. ★★★★★]
