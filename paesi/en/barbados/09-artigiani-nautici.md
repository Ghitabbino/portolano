# 09 — Marine Trades & Chandlers

Local hub is **limited**: Barbados is not a technical hub — for major work many owners rely on **regional hubs (Antigua — English Harbour/Jolly Harbour, Sint Maarten — Simpson Bay, Trinidad — Chaguaramas)**. In Bridgetown **freelancers work in the marina** (mechanics, electrics, composites) but without a structured network like Le Marin (Martinique). Contact is typically by phone/WhatsApp; English is spoken. For significant jobs always request a **written quote** and confirm lead times in high season (Dec–Apr), when the few trades are fully booked.

## Other Useful Operators (regional hubs for a serious refit)

- **Antigua — English Harbour (North Sails, Sanders Sails, Jolly Harbour marine services)**: sail lofts, riggers, composites — ~240 M NW, the nearest for structured work.
- **Sint Maarten — Simpson Bay (Budget Marine, FKG Composites, 100 t travelift)**: duty-free, immediate spares.
- **Trinidad — Chaguaramas (Power Boats, Peakes, Coral Cove, 70–300 t travelifts)**: outside the hurricane belt, hurricane hub.
- **Saint Lucia — Rodney Bay Marina**: intermediate services ~95 M away, good alternative to Bridgetown for a technical stop.
- In Barbados: **basic chandlery + town hardware stores** (Bay Street, Harbour Road); for paint/antifoul ask in the harbour.

## Where to Find What

- **Engines/refrigeration/welding**: freelancers working at Bridgetown Shallow Draught / Careenage — ask in the harbour office; often ex-fishermen with a workshop in St. Michael. ★★
- **Sails / rigging**: sailmaker **often not resident** — mobile service from Antigua/St. Martin on call (1–2 weeks). Keep a spare sail aboard. ★★
- **Antifouling/haul-out**: check **travelift/crane** availability in the marina — data not published; alternative is Chaguaramas/Antigua for hauling.
- **Liferafts / safety**: servicing at regional hubs — not documented in Barbados as of 28/08/2026.

## Practical Notes

- **Payment**: many trades accept **local BBD/USD bank transfer** or cash; for small jobs cash/card. Always ask for an **invoice** (useful for customs/insurance). If the job is >500 BBD ask for a **deposit only once work has started** and balance on completion.
- **Lead times**: spares from Europe/USA = **1–3 weeks** (DHL/Colissimo) + customs; workshops often order via Miami. High season (Dec–Apr) = workshops busy even in regional hubs — book weeks ahead.
- **On-board power**: electrical components (battery chargers, inverters, solar controllers) **limited availability** in Barbados — better to order before the Barbados leg or stock up in Antigua/St. Martin.
- **Antifoul**: available at the harbour hardware/chandlery and at Port St. Charles; a proper haul-out is done in Chaguaramas or Antigua if a certified travelift is needed.
- **For AMEL / French yachts**: local firms work on all makes; for original aluminium rigging and AMEL-specific spares contact **first** the Antigua hubs (riggers with AMEL experience) or Caraïbe Marine Martinique before the crossing.
