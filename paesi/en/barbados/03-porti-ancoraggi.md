# 03 — Ports & Marinas

**Last checked: 28/08/2026** · Coordinates in decimal degrees / N-W as per source · datum WGS84 · VHF 12 Bridgetown Signal, VHF 16 Port Control

Barbados has **very little yachting infrastructure** compared with the French Windwards: the only sheltered area is the **leeward west coast** (Carlisle Bay → Six Men's Bay). The Atlantic east coast is **not tenable at anchor** in normal conditions (surf, continuous swell). The cruisers' preferred anchorage is **Carlisle Bay** off Bridgetown; the marinas are **Bridgetown Shallow Draught / Careenage** (town centre) and the two resort marinas of the north-west (**Port St. Charles** — POE + **Port Ferdinand** — non-POE). Cruising sources aggregate ★★★ · [Barbados Port Inc.](https://barbadosport.com/vessel-documentation) ★★★★★

## Leeward West Coast — Bridgetown and Carlisle Bay ⚓ main area

### Bridgetown — Shallow Draught & Careenage (main harbour) ⚓

- **13°06.07' N 59°37.62' W (Shallow Draught, yacht basin)** · **13°06.00' N 59°37.18' W (inner Careenage)** · VHF **12** Signal Station ("8P") + VHF 16 · [Barbados Port Inc. — Vessel Documentation](https://barbadosport.com/vessel-documentation) ★★★★★ · [Marinas.com — Careenage](https://marinas.com/view/marina/7ec51w_Careenage_Marina_Bridgetown_Barbados) ★★
- **Shallow Draught**: sheltered basin *inside* the commercial harbour but separate, dedicated to yachts (~40 fully serviced berths, water/power, security). The only basin with **yacht-dedicated Customs/Immigration** and the recommended POE at Bridgetown. Depth adequate for yachts ("shallow" only for ships). Book via Harbour Master tel +1 246 434-6100. [BPI news 2024-12](https://barbadosport.com/news/page/4) ★★★★ · [BPI Yacht Masters Guide](https://barbadosport.com/barbados-yacht-masters-guide) ★★★★
- **Careenage (inner UNESCO channel)**: historic channel in the heart of Bridgetown with **~50 stern-to berths** on alongside quays, limited depth (shallow draught), inner lifting bridge (opening on request), Molehead light Fl R 10s 2M. Town atmosphere, walkable to the centre/markets. Book via Marine Services/Harbour Master +1 246 434-6100. [BPI Yacht Masters Guide](https://barbadosport.com/barbados-yacht-masters-guide) ★★★★ · [Yachting World 2017](https://www.yachtingworld.com/features/reinventing-barbados-caribbean-island-works-welcome-back-cruisers-71257) ★★★
- **Carlisle Bay (open roadstead off Bridgetown)**: **not a marina** but the **official post-clearance anchorage** over sand (4–14 m). Exposed to westerly swell during frontal passages, but sheltered from the ENE trades. Watch for jet skis by day and the marine park wrecks (6 snorkelling wrecks 12–55 m immediately to the south). Tariff for yachts <50 m **BBD 110 flat at Carlisle Bay** per 2026 guides (to be confirmed). [BPI Yacht Masters Guide](https://barbadosport.com/barbados-yacht-masters-guide) ★★★★ · Cruising sources aggregate ★★★
- Clearance on site (Shallow Draught or Passenger Terminal — see [01](01-clearance.md)). Call **Signal Station VHF 12 before entering**, berth will be assigned; Q flag. Hours **06:00–22:00 daily** at the Passenger Terminal according to reports — verify on VHF.
- Shelter: good lee from the trades; ground swell with N/NW swell during winter swells or a passing hurricane — check the local forecast before entering. Dinghy landing on sand: beach the tender, padlock it. ★★

### Carlisle Bay — Public Anchorage

- **13°04.69' N 59°37.66' W (centre of the bay, ~13.078 -59.627)** · sand, 5–14 m, good holding. See card [anc-carlisle-main](ancoraggi/anc-carlisle-main.md). This is where almost all cruisers stay after clearance, a few minutes by dinghy from the boatyard and town centre.

## North-West Coast — Port St. Charles & Port Ferdinand (resort, sheltered)

### Port St. Charles Marina — Northern POE ⚓

- **13°15.83' N 59°38.79' W (lagoon entrance, outer anchorage 13°15.50' N 59°38.47' W)** · VHF **16** (calling) · [Port St. Charles](https://www.portstcharles.com) · [Marinas.com — Port St. Charles](https://marinas.com/view/marina/n8cexq_Port_St_Charles_Marina_St_Peter_Barbados) ★★ · [Navily — Port St. Charles](https://www.navily.com/port/port-st-charles-marina/1967) ★★★
- **Official northern POE** (second POE of the island): customs/immigration/coast guard/police **in the same building at the end of the breakwater**; procedure quick and friendly per report 30/11/2025 (anchor outside, dinghy to the inner pontoon). **6 mega-yacht berths up to 250 ft / 76 m, max draught ~4.1 m (13.5 ft)** on the outer breakwater; duty-free fuel on clearance (cash); **no toilets/showers/laundry** on the mega-yacht quays, but resort pool/restaurant at the end of the breakwater. Cruising sources aggregate ★★★ · [Minty Yachts — Barbados](https://www.mintedyachts.com/locations/barbados) ★★
- Outer anchorage over **3–4 m sand**, good holding, calm except for occasional swell; watch for **turtles** in the area. See card [anc-psc-anch](ancoraggi/anc-psc-anch.md). Practical: enter the marina only on berth assignment; for clearance just tie up at the customs pontoon.

### Port Ferdinand Marina (St. Peter) — Luxury Resort, NOT a POE

- **13°15.51' N 59°38.70' W (inner basin, outer anchorage Six Men's Bay)** · [Port Ferdinand](https://www.portferdinand.com) ★★ · [Yachting World 2017](https://www.yachtingworld.com/features/reinventing-barbados-caribbean-island-works-welcome-back-cruisers-71257) ★★★ · [Marinas.com — Port Ferdinand](https://marinas.com/view/marina/z4cr829_Port_Ferdinand_St_Peter_Barbados) ★★
- **NOT a POE**: formalities at Bridgetown or Port St. Charles; a **coastal permit** from the Customs Boarding Officer (tel +1 246 430-2355, VHF 16) is needed to move along the coast without fresh clearance if already cleared in. [BPI Yacht Masters Guide §8](https://barbadosport.com/barbados-yacht-masters-guide) ★★★★
- Basin **cut into the rock**, 120–126 berths, suitable for superyachts, entrance via Six Men's Bay **under a lifting bridge** (watch your air draught). Large yachts often anchor outside in the roadstead. Full resort facilities (13°/59° restaurant, beach club, spa). See card [anc-port-ferdinand](ancoraggi/anc-port-ferdinand.md).

## Central West Coast — Speightstown, Holetown, Paynes Bay (open roadsteads)

- **Speightstown (13°15.18' N 59°38.70' W)** — roadstead over sand off the town, roll present, dinghy landing limited; no customs; coastal permit as above. Shops/supermarket walkable. See [anc-speightstown](ancoraggi/anc-speightstown.md). [noforeignland](https://www.noforeignland.com/guides/countries/bb) ★★
- **Holetown / Paynes Bay / Sandy Lane** — long leeward bays, anchoring over sand 3–8 m, good holding but open to westerly swell. Busy with day charters, possible private hotel buoys — **do not use without permission**. Dinghy landing on the beach, watch the surf when a swell is running. ★★

## South Coast — Oistins, Silver Sands (exposed, conditional)

- **Oistins Bay (13°03.93' N 59°32.47' W)** — bay open to the **south** with a roadstead over 3–8 m sand, exposed to south Atlantic swell; tenable **only in calm conditions** (Dec–Apr best). Famous for the **Friday Fish Fry** and fish market, but **not a reliable refuge** in fresh trades. Many local fishing boats. See [anc-oistins](ancoraggi/anc-oistins.md). [Navily — Oistins](https://www.navily.com/mouillage/oistins-fischerort/48071) ★★
- **St. Lawrence Gap / Worthing / Hastings** — tourist shoreline with fringing reef, sand/coral bottom, anchoring only in settled weather and never overnight without a watch; respect swimming corridors. ★★

## Atlantic East Coast — NOT Tenable (Bathsheba, Tent Bay, Consett Bay)

- **Bathsheba / Tent Bay (13°14.82' N 59°32.17' W)** and **Consett Bay** — **to be excluded as anchorages** in normal conditions: **windward** coast with **continuous Atlantic swell**, depths 4–10 m but no shelter. Only local fishing boats; marked only as **to be AVOIDED**. See [anc-bathsheba](ancoraggi/anc-bathsheba.md). [safetyanchoralarm.com — South & East Coast](https://safetyanchoralarm.com/anchorages/barbados/barbados-south-east) ★★
