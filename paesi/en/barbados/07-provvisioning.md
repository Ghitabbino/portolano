**Last checked: 28/08/2026**

## General Price Level — Barbados

- Currency: **Barbados dollar (BBD, pegged 2:1 to USD)** — groceries are **imported and more expensive than in mainland Europe (+30–50%)**; local produce (fish, tropical fruit, bread) is better value. [skiptomalouuu.com](https://www.skiptomalouuu.com/blog/best-eateries-in-barbados) ★★★
- Yacht logistics: for **ocean passages** it is better to stock up in the larger islands (Antigua, St. Martin, Trinidad) where there is large-scale duty-free distribution; Barbados has **good urban distribution** (Massy, Trimart, Cost U Less) but higher prices for imports. ★★

### Details for Main Base — Bridgetown Careenage / Shallow Draught

- **Daily shopping on foot is feasible** from Harbour Road (Massy ~500 m): folding trolley recommended; Worthing/Trimart/Cost U Less are for the **weekly shop** (taxi 20–40 BBD each way, agree fare beforehand).
- **Typical supermarket hours**: **08:00–20:00 Mon–Sat, 08:00–14:00 Sun/holidays** (Massy); local minimarts often **07:00–21:00 daily**. Reduced hours on public holidays (Crop Over, Christmas). ★★
- **Delivery aboard**: rare, only on request from the larger supermarkets equipped for yacht provisioning (Massy Worthing/Holetown catering); confirm by phone/email with 24h notice. ★★
- **Chandlery near the marina** for basic yacht spares; prices reasonable versus the resort. ★★

## Markets

- **Bridgetown Fisheries Complex (Princess Alice Hwy, south of the Careenage)**: **fish market** daily with fresh catch in the morning (flying fish, dolphin/mahi-mahi, tuna, local barracuda); stalls open 06:00–16:00, busiest early morning after the fishing boats return. ★★★
- **Cheapside Market (Bridgetown)** + **Browne's Beach / Fairchild Market**: local fruit/veg early morning (breadfruit, sweet potato, green banana, seasonal mango); covered market with spices. ★★★
- **Oistins Fish Market / Bay Garden (Christ Church)**: famous **Friday Fish Fry** but also a daily market with fresh fish; fruit trucks along Highway 7 and at Six Roads. ★★★
- Where there is no proper covered market, **roadside stalls and fruit trucks** — good local prices. ★★

## Transport for Provisioning

### Taxis

- Service is **not metered** on most of the island: **agree the fare before boarding**. Fares are regulated by Barbados Tourism with an official list for tourist routes. ★★★
- Typical fare **Careenage/Shallow Draught ↔ Massy Harbour Road**: **10–20 BBD (~5–10 USD)**; **Careenage ↔ Worthing/Trimart**: **40–60 BBD**; **Oistins ↔ Bridgetown**: **50–70 BBD**; **Holetown ↔ Speightstown**: **30–50 BBD** — confirm with driver or harbour office. ★★
- Taxi contact: ask in the marina/harbour office or at the supermarket; taxi ranks at Harbour Road, Bridgetown Bus Terminal, airport. Central number Barbados Taxi Association **+1 246 426-1021** (to be verified as of 28/08/2026) — **DATA MISSING** if no answer, use hotel taxi.

### Bus / ZR / Minibus

- **Blue government buses (BTMI) + white ZRs with red stripe (route taxis)**: very frequent on main routes (Bridgetown ↔ Holetown/Speightstown via Highway 1; Bridgetown ↔ Oistins via Highway 7), every **5–15 min** by day, fare **3.50 BBD (~1.75 USD)** per ride (flat fare since 2020, exact change). [BTMI](https://www.transportboard.com) ★★★
- Nearest stop to the marina: **Princess Alice Hwy / Harbour Road** 100–300 m from the Careenage/Shallow Draught; for Port St. Charles stop **Highway 1 at Six Men's Bay** (100 m). ★★
- Inconvenient with heavy loads: better to take a taxi for water cases or the weekly shop; bus useful for reconnaissance without loads.

## Practical Tips

- **Folding trolley + insulated bags**: essential if shopping on foot with the dinghy (beach landing at Carlisle, quayside at the Careenage).
- **Bottled water**: stock up where dock water is doubtful or non-potable; keep **bagged ice** for passages to Saint Lucia/Grenada.
- Always check **holiday hours and ice availability** before long passages (180–240 M).
- For **ocean provisioning** (transatlantic): best to do one big shop at **Bridgetown (Massy Harbour Road + Cost U Less Warrens)** by taxi; for daily top-ups use **Holetown/Speightstown** if anchored in the north.
