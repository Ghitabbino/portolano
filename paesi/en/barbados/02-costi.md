# 02 — Cost of Living

**Last updated: 28/08/2026**

Currency: **Barbados dollar (BBD, Bds$)** — pegged at **2 BBD = 1 USD**; prices are shown in BBD everywhere, US dollars are accepted almost everywhere at the fixed rate; the island is **heavily import-dependent**, groceries are **30–50% more expensive than in mainland Europe/continental USA**, tourist restaurants are pricey in marina areas (as local guides put it: "comparable to New York/London"). ## Cash & Payments

**BBD pegged 2:1 to USD**: prices are shown in BBD; almost everyone accepts US cash at the fixed rate (change in BBD). Cards (Visa/Mastercard) are accepted in marinas, supermarkets and tourist restaurants; cash is essential for buses/markets/fish fries/Oistins. ATMs in Bridgetown (Broad Street, Harbour Road), Holetown (Limegrove), Oistins and the airport. High fees for withdrawals with foreign cards.

> At the Oistins fish fries and beach stalls many vendors prefer **cash in BBD/USD** — keep small notes.
