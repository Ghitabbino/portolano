# 04 — Services, Boatyards & Maintenance

**Last checked: 28/08/2026**

## Main Hub — Bridgetown / Shallow Draught

- Services are concentrated in the **Bridgetown harbour/marina** (Shallow Draught + Careenage); for major work the marina/Bay Street area is the reference. Barbados is **not a Caribbean technical hub**: infrastructure is limited compared with Antigua, St. Martin or Trinidad. ★★
- **Travelift / crane / haul-out**: **DATA MISSING** for verified capacity in Barbados — ask the Harbour Master +1 246 434-6100 or Port St. Charles +1 246 419-1000. For structural refits many owners rely on **Antigua (English Harbour/Jolly Harbour), Sint Maarten (Simpson Bay) or Trinidad (Chaguaramas)** with 70–300 t travelifts and dozens of trades. [Yachting World 2017 — Barbados](https://www.yachtingworld.com/features/reinventing-barbados-caribbean-island-works-welcome-back-cruisers-71257) ★★★
- **Chandlery**: **basic** range locally (hardware, anchors, rope, paint); for specific spares (Volvo Penta, Yanmar, NKE/Raymarine electronics) allow **1–3 weeks** lead time via Miami/St. Martin/UK. Shipping: DHL/Colissimo; duty payable on arrival. ★★
- **Hull care**: **scrubbing and painting is possible in the Bridgetown basins** according to local reports, but **DATA MISSING** for a certified travelift/crane in Barbados — check with the harbour offices before planning. ★★

## Other Useful Operators (regional hubs recommended for a serious refit)

- **Antigua — English Harbour / Jolly Harbour**: 70 t travelift, dozens of trades (mechanics, composites, North/Sanders sails), the nearest hub east of Barbados for major work. ★★★
- **Sint Maarten — Simpson Bay Lagoon**: Budget Marine, FKG, multiple yards, 100 t+ travelifts, duty-free. ★★★★
- **Trinidad — Chaguaramas (Peakes, Power Boats, Coral Cove)**: travelifts up to 300 t, the best-equipped hurricane hub in the southern Caribbean. ★★★★
- Locally in Bridgetown: **basic chandlery** + town hardware stores; for composite/fibreglass work ask in the harbour — limited availability.

## Gas & Bottles

- **Gas/LPG: often bottle exchange only** for local/US standards (e.g. Campingaz 904/907 or US 20 lb). Cylinders are **not refilled, they are exchanged** at LPG points or hardware stores; check US vs EU regulator compatibility (adapter required). Cruising sources aggregate ★★★
- Supermarkets/chandlery in Bridgetown stock adapters and Campingaz cylinders; for a true refill you need to move to a larger hub. ★★

## Fresh Water

- **Dock water**: potable according to the **BWA (Barbados Water Authority)**, desalinated in the Bridgetown area; however **DATA MISSING** on certified potability at the Shallow Draught pontoon — ask in the marina and consider **carbon filtering** or a watermaker. Mains pressurised but **pressure drops possible** in high season — keep reserves. ★★
- Water cost: **DATA MISSING** rate/m³ in Barbados as of 28/08/2026 — ask in the harbour offices → [03 Ports](03-porti-ancoraggi.md).

## Strategic Notes

- Barbados **is not a refit island**: if the job can be planned (standing rigging, sails, engine, osmosis), **schedule it in Antigua/St. Martin/Trinidad** before or after the Barbados stop; keep the Barbados call for stores/clearance/light technical stops. This is confirmed by all guides 2017–2025 describing it as "basic yachting facilities, good repair facilities but improving" — not a hub. Cruising sources aggregate ★★★ · [Yachting World](https://www.yachtingworld.com/features/reinventing-barbados-caribbean-island-works-welcome-back-cruisers-71257) ★★★
- **Spares**: have them shipped **to the marina stating yacht name, berth and phone**; confirm arrival via email/phone with reception; duty payable online or dockside. Lead time EU/USA → Barbados **7–14 days** by courier; allow for customs. ★★
