# 00 — Entry, Documents & Visas

**Last checked: 28/08/2026**

## Status

Barbados is an **independent parliamentary republic** since 30 November 2021 (former Commonwealth realm), a member of the Commonwealth, **outside the EU and outside the Schengen Area**: it has its own customs and immigration and is not part of the OECS. EU/Schengen rules do not apply. [immigration.gov.bb](https://immigration.gov.bb/pages/visitor.aspx) ★★★★★ · [gov.bb — Immigration Department](https://www.gov.bb/Departments/immigration) ★★★★★

## Important Note for Arrival by Sea

- **People** (Immigration) and **yacht** (Customs + Port Health + Port Authority) controls are **separate but linked**: the yacht must be presented at an **official Port of Entry** and the crew must have completed the **online Immigration & Customs Form**. Yacht clearance is mandatory on entry and on exit → see [01 — Clearance](01-clearance.md).
- **Ports of Entry (POE) for yachts: Bridgetown** (Shallow Draught dedicated to yachts + Deep Water Harbour on instruction) **and Port St. Charles Marina** (NW, clearance directly in the marina at the end of the breakwater). **Do not approach the cruise/commercial wharf** with a vessel <20 m: dangerous, no dinghy dock — better to stand off and proceed by dinghy to the Passenger Terminal on the instructions of the Signal Station. [Barbados Port Inc. — Vessel Documentation](https://barbadosport.com/vessel-documentation) ★★★★★ · Cruising sources aggregate ★★★ · Cruising sources aggregate ★★★
- **Approaching Bridgetown**: call **Bridgetown Signal Station on VHF 12 (callsign "8P")** before entering; a berth will be assigned; fly a clearly visible **yellow Q flag** on entry. Usual anchorage after clearance is **Carlisle Bay (5–10 m over sand)**, then dinghy/taxi to the offices. The vessel is normally **boarded** by Port Health + Customs + Immigration (~10 min for Free Pratique); the vessel's own forms are accepted. Required: ship's particulars, **list of the last 10 ports visited**, crew and passenger lists, zarpe from the previous port.
- **Approaching Port St. Charles**: anchor outside over sand off the marina, then dinghy to the inner pontoon; officers are **in the same building at the end of the breakwater** (customs/immigration/coast guard/police) — procedure described as quick and friendly (Cruising sources aggregate report 30/11/2025). Cruising sources aggregate ★★★
- **Digital pre-arrival**: **SailClear** ([sailclear.com](https://www.sailclear.com)) is recommended as a customs pre-notification (Barbados has been a SailClear CCLEC country since 2013); it does not replace presenting in person at the dock but speeds up the paperwork; SailClear has charged an annual fee of US$25 since 01/05/2024. [SailClear](https://www.sailclear.com) ★★★ · [CCLEC — SailClear](https://www.cclec.org/about-us/sailclear/) ★★★
