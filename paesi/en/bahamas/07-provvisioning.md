# 07 — Provisioning

**Last checked: 24/08/2026**

## General Price Level

Shopping **expensive: +20–80% vs USA** due to imports + **VAT 10%** (12% on some restaurant products/services). Local products (fresh fish, conch) cheaper; imported fruit/veg expensive and perishable outside Nassau. Best to **do big provisioning in Florida** and top up fresh in the cays. ★★★★

- Bags chargeable, better to have a folding trolley/cart on board.
- Sunday many shops closed or half-day outside Nassau — plan ahead.

### Transport — Nassau / Paradise Island

**Taxi:**
- Hub / dispatch Nassau: **242-323-3030 / 242-325-3333** — fare **not metered, agree beforehand**. Short ride Bay Street–Paradise **$15–25**, airport–downtown **$30–35** + baggage supplement ($2/bag) ★★★. From Hurricane Hole/Atlantis to Harbour Bay Solomon's **$18–28 return** estimated. Ask for fixed round-trip price with waiting (30 min shopping) ★★.
- App: no official Uber; hotel/marina taxis call on the spot (5–10 min wait).

**Jitney (local bus):**
- New Providence only. Public network **#1, #8, #10, #10A** pass East Bay / Bay Street / Harbour Bay. Stop **on request on Bay Street / East Bay Street** (minimal yellow pole/sign) ★★. Frequency **every 10–20 min** Mon–Sat 6:30–19, Sunday reduced (6:30–18, every 20–30 min) ★★. Fare **$1.25–1.50 / ride** cash on board, no tourist pass ★★★. Not for heavy loads; awkward with water cases. Source: 02-costs ★★★ + local reports.

**Ferry (to provision and move crew):**
- **Bahamas Ferries** from **Potter's Cay Dock (Western End), Nassau** — tel **242-323-2166 ext. 250** / reservations@bahamasferries.com — connects **Harbour Island, Eleuthera (Governor's Harbour, Rock Sound), Marsh Harbour (Abaco), Exuma**. Island family offices: Freeport 242-807-8018, Exuma 242-808-0658, Marsh Harbour 242-808-0581 ★★★★. Useful if crew wants to shop in Nassau and return to cays via ferry with luggage (but baggage limit). Fare **DATA MISSING** — book at booking.bahamasferries.com.
- **Fast Ferry / mail boat** for northern Exumas (Highbourne→ Nassau) irregular — **DATA MISSING**, ask at marina.

## Local Markets

- **Potter's Cay (Nassau)**: daily fresh fish — best for conch/grouper. Recommended arrival 7–9 am. ★★★★★
- **Fruit/veg markets**: small stalls at Nassau (Fox Hill, Arawak Cay), Marsh Harbour, George Town — local seasonal produce (mango, papaya, banana, lime). ★★
- **Direct fishing**: ask fishermen at Georgetown Fish Dock or Staniel — conch cleaned on the spot, lobster (in season).

## Practical Tips (Martinique Standard)

- **Strategic provisioning**: bulk in **Florida or Nassau (Solomon's/Super Value/Cost Right Harbour Bay)**, fresh at **Potter's Cay** and cays (Pinder's, Maxwell's, Exuma Markets). Don't leave under-provisioned for central Exumas. ★★★★
- **Taxi vs Jitney**: with heavy loads **always agreed taxi**; jitney only for light reconnaissance in Nassau. Keep **small USD notes** ($1, $5) for jitney and markets.
- **Water**: carry **extra jerry cans 20–40 L** for central Exumas — between Highbourne and George Town water points are sparse and expensive.
- **Delivery aboard**: Solomon's Harbour Bay/Old Fort and Exuma Markets offer delivery — order **24h ahead by phone/online shop** and have delivered to marina (check time and minimum). Sawyer's inside Hurricane Hole is already aboard — no delivery needed.
- **Cash**: in remote cays many shops take only **cash USD/BSD** — keep small notes; Visa/Mastercard ok at Nassau/Atlantis/Marsh Harbour/Georgetown ★★★.
- **Hours**: Sunday many shops closed or half-day outside Nassau — plan; Solomon's Harbour Bay Sunday 7–18, Old Fort 7–20.
- **Trolley**: folding trolley essential from Bay Street Marina to Potter's Cay and from Boat Harbour to Maxwell's.
