# 01 — Clearance

**Last checked: 24/08/2026** · Main source: Bahamas Customs & Bahamas Maritime Authority — **Click2Clear portal (besw.gov.bs)** ★★★★★

## Who Must Clear

All pleasure vessels **entering** Bahamian waters from abroad must clear at a **designated Port of Entry**. The procedure has been **digital only since 22/02/2021** via Click2Clear. No paper form at the dock any more. ★★★★★

## Click2Clear Procedure — Online Only

1. Register at **https://besw.gov.bs** (Bahamas Electronic Single Window) and create your **Click2Clear** account before arrival. ★★★★★
2. Fill in the **Pleasure Craft Declaration**: vessel details (name, registration, flag, LOA, draught), owner/skipper, complete **crew manifest** (passports), weapons and pets on board (if any), previous/next port, ETA. ★★★★★
3. Submit the declaration: the system generates a **Rotation Number**. ★★★★★
4. **Online payment** by **Visa** (only card accepted online) or declaration of **cash payment on arrival** — WARNING: **approval is not issued until payment is credited**; if you choose "cash on arrival" you must pay at Customs and wait for release. ★★★★★
5. Print/save to your phone the **approved Cruising Permit** with QR/Rotation Number.

> Useful tip: your Click2Clear profile saves vessel and crew — for subsequent exits/re-entries simply **duplicate** the previous application and update only dates and port. ★★★

## Arrival — What to Do at Sea and Ashore

| Phase | Rule | |
|---|---|---|
| Approaching | Fly **Q flag (yellow)** and call the chosen **Designated Port of Entry** on **VHF 16**; ask for instructions for the customs berth | ★★★★★ |
| Ashore | **Skipper only goes ashore** — crew stays on board until clearance is completed | ★★★★★ |
| Documents to present | **Passports** for all crew, **cruising permit / Click2Clear Rotation Number**, **vessel registration/ownership document**, **insurance** (if available), any **weapons/pets permits**, printed crew list | ★★★★★ |
| Departure (outbound clearance) | **Outbound clearance** mandatory if required by the authorities of the next country (zarpe) — fee **$75** | ★★★★ |

## Weapons on Board — Strict Rules

| Item | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Declaration | **Mandatory** on Click2Clear and to the officer — weapons kept **locked on board**, not to be taken ashore | Bahamas Customs / Police ★★★★★ |
| Quantity limits | **Max 3 firearms** per vessel, calibre **≤ .308**, max **250 rounds** total | Bahamas Police ★★★★★ |
| Prohibitions | **Automatic weapons prohibited**, assault weapons, **open center console with weapons** — possession forbidden in that case | Bahamas Police ★★★★ |
| Consequence of violation | Seizure of weapon + vessel, arrest, fine | Bahamas Police ★★★★★ |

## Pets

| Item | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| BAHFSA import permit | **Pre-approved permit mandatory** from the **Bahamas Agriculture Health and Food Safety Authority (BAHFSA)** — fee **$11.20** per application | BAHFSA ★★★★★ |
| Requirements | **Microchip**, valid **rabies** vaccination, **veterinary certificate within 48h** of departure, up-to-date vaccinations | BAHFSA ★★★★★ |
| Banned breeds | **Pit Bull, Presa Canario, Cane Corso, American Bully** and crosses — **entry banned** | BAHFSA ★★★★★ |
| On board | Pet declared, stays on board until authorised; inspection possible | BAHFSA ★★★★ |

### AIS & Penalties

- **AIS mandatory** for vessels **≥50 ft (15.24 m)** — must remain on in Bahamian waters. **Fine $1,000** if non-compliant. ★★★★★
- **Penalties for failure to clear / non-payment / AIS off**: fines, **vessel seizure**, possible arrest. ★★★★★

## To Check Before Your Cruise

- Click2Clear portal working and Visa card enabled for foreign payments.
- Chosen port of entry on VHF 16 and Customs/Immigration hours — weekend = overtime.
- Weapons/pets permits requested **before** departure.
- Latest FDCC/Anchorage fee circular (frequent updates since 2025).
