# 04 — Services, Boatyards & Maintenance

**Last checked: 24/08/2026**

## Main Hubs — Where the Yard Is

The Bahamas are **not a cheap boatyard hub** like Florida or Martinique: few travelifts, high prices, almost all spares imported from the USA. Useful hubs are **Freeport (Grand Bahama)**, **Nassau (New Providence)** and **Spanish Wells (Eleuthera)** for haul-out; for complex work many owners return to Florida. ★★★★

### Grand Bahama — Industrial Hub

#### Bradford Marine — Freeport ★★★★★
- **Dry dock 1,200 T**, **Travelift 150 T**, full yard for superyacht and sailing refit, painting, mechanics, electrics. Contact via bradford-marine.com — quote mandatory. ★★★★★

#### Grand Bahama Shipyard — Freeport ★★★★★
- **Dock 27,600 T** for ships and mega-yachts (3 floating docks), hull/steel work, propellers, engines — industrial scale, shipyard pricing. ★★★★★

### Eleuthera — Traditional Storage

#### R&B Boatyard — Spanish Wells ★★★★
- **Railway / slip 90 ft (27 m), 120 T** — historic yard for dry storage, bottom jobs, paint, small wood/fibreglass repairs. Family-run, more reasonable prices than Nassau. ★★★★

### Nassau — Afloat Services & Light Bottom Work

#### Brown's Boat Basin — Nassau ★★★★
- **Travelift 40 T** — haul-out, bottom, antifouling, basic mechanics. East Bay Street, Nassau. ★★★★

#### Brown's Boat Basin — Exuma (Satellite) ★★★
- **10,000 lbs (4.5 T)** — tenders / small boats only at George Town. ★★★

#### Compass Marine — Nassau ★★★★
- **24/7 emergency**, dealer **MTU / Cummins / Volvo Penta / Yanmar** — diagnostics, engine spares, electrics. Onboard interventions at marina or at anchor via workboat. ★★★★

> Other scattered artisans: electricians, refrigeration technicians and riggers freelance between Nassau, Marsh Harbour and George Town — ask at the marina office; **always get a written quote** before work starts. ★★

## Gas & Bottles

- **LPG/Gas: US standard bottles** — refills possible at some points (DATA MISSING per individual cay), otherwise **bottle swap** like US Blue Rhino. US↔EU adapters needed for European boats. ★★
- Check **fitting compatibility** before departure from Florida. ★★

## Fresh Water

- **RO desalinated** water everywhere: **$0.10–0.25 / gal** at Nassau/Georgetown, **$0.40–0.50 / gal** at Highbourne/Staniel (see zone details in [07 Provisioning](07-provvisioning.md)). No structural rationing like in Martinique, but in **remote cays (Exumas)** availability depends on the marina watermaker — keep a reserve. Water marina tel: Hurricane Hole VHF 11, Highbourne 242-355-1009, Staniel 242-355-2024. ★★★★

## Emergency & Rescue at Sea

| Service | Contact |
|---|---|
| **BASRA (Bahamas Air Sea Rescue Association)** | **242-325-8864** (HQ Nassau) · **VHF 16** |
| **Police / General Emergency** | **919** (also **911 / 112** works from mobile) ★★★★ |
| **Distress Call** | **VHF 16 — PAN PAN / MAYDAY** — BASRA + Harbour Control Nassau H24 |

BASRA is **voluntary and donation-based** — like the British RNLI: donations welcome, interventions free. No equivalent of the French CROSS. ★★★★

## Strategic Notes

- **Spares**: order from USA (West Marine, Defender, Amazon US) shipped to **Nassau (freight forwarder)** or **Florida** — Bahamas customs **+ VAT 10% + duty 10–45%** by category. Lead time 1–3 weeks. ★★★
- **Antifouling**: available in Nassau/Freeport — US brands (Interlux, Pettit) — prices +30% vs USA. ★★
- **Sails / Standing Rigging**: no major permanent loft — **DATA MISSING** for local sailmaker; rely on Florida for new sails. ★★
- For work under 50 ft, **R&B Spanish Wells** is the best value for money in the central Bahamas. ★★★
