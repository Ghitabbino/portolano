# 02 — Cost of Living

**Last updated: 27/08/2026**

Currency: **Aruban florin (AWG, Afl.)** pegged at **1 USD = 1.79 AWG (1.80 in shops/taxis)**; US dollars are accepted everywhere, change is often given in florins. Price level: almost everything is imported — groceries **+20–40%** vs. mainland USA/continental Europe, tourist restaurants expensive around the marinas. ★★★ [Numbeo Oranjestad Jun 2026; gobierno.aw; taxi.aw/visitaruba.com ★★★★★]

> ⚠️ The items in this list are the same for every country in the pilot. Where data has not yet been found the table shows **DATA MISSING**: it is researched at the monthly check (see [00 Index](00-indice.md)).

## Cash & Payments

Aruban florin and US dollars accepted everywhere (restaurants, marinas, supermarkets); Visa/Mastercard accepted in almost all tourist businesses and marinas. Cash useful for buses, markets, small kiosks and tips. ATMs in Oranjestad, at the airport and in tourist areas (Palm/Eagle Beach, San Nicolas). Reference fixed rate **1 USD = 1.79 AWG** (1.80 in taxi/shop tariffs). [visitaruba.com](https://www.visitaruba.com/getting-around/taxis-and-limos) ★★★★★
