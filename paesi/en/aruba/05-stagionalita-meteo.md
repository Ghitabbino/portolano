# 05 — Seasonality & Weather

**Last checked: 27/08/2026**

## Climate

- Average temperature **28.5 °C** (annual mean), 27.3 °C Dec–Feb and 29.5 °C Aug–Oct; **hot semi-arid steppe BSh/BW** climate of the southern Dry Zone, moderate to fresh E trades. ★★★★★
- Average rainfall **~498 mm/year** highly variable (years <200 mm and >1000 mm); sea temperature 25.9 °C Feb–Mar and 28.2 °C Sep–Oct; daily range greater than seasonal range. ★★★★★

## Seasons

| Period | Characteristics |
|---|---|
| **February – June** | Dry season, steady trades and mostly clear skies: **best period for cruising** |
| **September – January** | **Rainy season** with brief showers in the morning/early evening; peak Oct–Dec; Jul–Sep transition |

★★★

## Hurricane Procedures (official ★★★★)

Aruba has no French-style cyclone plan; follow **alerts from the Meteorological Department Aruba (DMA)** and instructions from the **harbour master/Port Control VHF 16**; Varadero in the lagoon is the preferred shelter but no berth is 100% guaranteed in a cyclone.

## Hurricanes

Season **1 June–30 November**, peak mid-August–mid-October. Aruba lies on the **southern edge of the hurricane belt**: major damage from a cyclone passing to the south about **once in 100 years**; effects possible from systems to the north (erosion, heavy swell, coastal flooding). Hurricanes with trailing impact: Hazel (1954), Ivan (2004, waves and flooding), Felix (2007, centre 80 km away). [DMA meteo.aw ★★★★★; meteo.cw ★★★★★; aruba.com ★★★]

## Local Winds

- Trades from **E/ENE 12–20 kt**, average 7.6 m/s stronger in the dry/transition season and lighter in the wet season; acceleration gusts between the ABC islands and in the lee of low hills. ★★★★★
- Saharan dust (SAL) and Atlantic high pressure can reduce visibility and bring seas from E–NW 4–7 ft with small-craft warnings on exposed coasts. ★★★★

## Typical Weather Windows

Short passages Aruba–Curaçao (~68 NM) or Aruba–Bonaire (~105 NM) feasible with trades ≤18–20 kt, best departing at dawn; for long passages to Colombia/Venezuela wait for a window after a tropical wave with stable pressure. Inter-island channels: confused seas — avoid wind >20 kt. ★★
