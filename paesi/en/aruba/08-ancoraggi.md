# 08 — Anchorage Pilot

**Last checked: 27/08/2026 · indicative coordinates, navigate with official chart**

## General Anchoring Rules (First and Foremost)

- **Free anchoring** is permitted on the leeward side (west-south coast) only on **sand**, EXCEPT the prohibited zones below and the **coral/seagrass** bottoms protected by **Parke Marino Aruba** (anchoring on coral prohibited, use only sand or buoys where present). ★★★★
- **IALA-B** throughout the Caribbean: red to starboard when entering harbour (reversed vs. Europe). Always call **Port Control VHF 16** for entry into the Oranjestad channel and for any movement inside the basin. ★★★★
- **Barrier reef**: always keep >0.5 NM off the drying reef at the north-west (Malmok/Arashi), enter only in good light following the 10 m contour; at night only with verified waypoints and echo sounder. ★★

### ⛔ Absolute No-Go Zones (Official)

| Area | Prohibition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Parke Marino Aruba — MPA Arikok, Mangel Halto, Sero Colorado, Oranjestad Reef Islands** | Anchoring prohibited on coral/seagrass and shallows; in many areas buoys mandatory once installed (AB 2018 no.77) | [FPNA](https://www.arubanationalpark.org/main/management-plan-marine-park/) ★★★★ |
| **Barrier reef and reef islets** | Anchoring or laying chain on coral prohibited; tying to mangroves prohibited | AB 2018 no.77 art.9c ★★★★ |
| **Oranjestad channel and basin** | Anchoring prohibited in the dredged channel and cruise basin; berthing only on Port Control instructions | [Aruba Ports Authority](https://www.arubaports.com) ★★★★★ |
| **Barcadera channel** | Anchoring prohibited in the commercial channel; transit only with authorisation VHF 16/11 | Aruba Ports Authority ★★★★★ · Cruising sources aggregate ★★★ |
| **Private buoys and swim zones** | Charter/hotel buoys and beach swim corridors not usable without permission; respect swim markings | aruba-cruisingguide.com ★★★ |
| **Bucuti Shoal** | Shoal 1.9 m between charter buoys and Bucuti Yacht Club — do not cross outside the channel | aruba-cruisingguide.com ★★★ |

Full texts: [FPNA — Management Plan Marine Park](https://www.arubanationalpark.org/main/management-plan-marine-park/) and Ramsar sheet [Parke Marino Aruba](https://rsis.ramsar.org) (no-anchor policy). ★★★★

- The depths of the Oranjestad (12.5' entrance) and Barcadera (2.2 m for Varadero) channels must be verified with **Aruba Ports Authority NtMs** before entering. ★★★★★
- For day-to-day navigation: official electronics on plotter + local guides as backup; **IALA-B** buoyage everywhere. ★★★
- **Beware Bucuti shoal**: from charter buoys to Bucuti Yacht Club only 1.9 m (6.5 ft) — follow the inner channel and do not cut across the shoal. ★★★

## Anchor Checklist for Aruba

1. Clearance at **Barcadera** done? → [01 Clearance](01-clearance.md) · VHF 16 30' before arrival
2. Waypoints for Oranjestad channel (SE buoy 12°30.317' N 70°02.153' W) and Varadero Channel (12°29.577' N 70°01.133' W) loaded on plotter
3. BA/NV chart up to date + IALA-B (red to starboard when entering) in mind
4. Bottom: only **sand** — never coral (Parke Marino), check good holding after dropping
5. Charter/hotel buoys avoided (do not use 09:00–17:00 at Antilla, others only with permission)
6. Dinghy padlocked, outboard secured — theft possible on docks at night
