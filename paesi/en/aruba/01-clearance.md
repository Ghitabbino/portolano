# 01 — Yacht Customs Clearance

**Last checked: 27/08/2026** · Main source: Aruba Ports Authority — Yachting (arubaports.com) ★★★★★ + Varadero Aruba Entry Procedure ★★★ + edcardaruba.aw ★★★★★

## Who Must Clear

All pleasure craft, regardless of flag and nationality, that:
- **enter** Aruban waters arriving from a foreign port/foreign territorial waters or from the high seas;
- **depart** for a foreign port/foreign waters or the high seas.
Clearance is mandatory even if you intend only to transit or make a brief stop: **anchoring or heading to a marina before completing immigration and customs at the port of entry is prohibited**. [arubaports.com Yachting — ENTRY PROCEDURES ★★★★★; varaderoaruba.com "ALL BOATS must go clear … at Barcadera" ★★★; aruba-cruisingguide.com ★★★]

Crew must **remain on board** until clearance is completed (yellow area / H Pier). [arubaports.com ★★★★★]

## Online Procedure

Aruba **has no single portal** like the French Antilles' "démarche-simplifiée": the digital part is **advance notice** + **ED Card for persons**, the rest is paper on the quay.

1. **Advance notice for the yacht — SailClear**: Customs uses [SailClear](https://www.sailclear.com) — complete it online **before arrival** (yacht details, owner, crew, last/next port, ETA, flag, MMSI, dimensions, engines, fuel). It does not replace the physical presence on the quay but speeds up processing. [Cruising sources aggregate Aruba "Customs use SailClear" ★★★; aruba-cruisingguide.com ★★★]
2. **Advance notice for persons — ED Card**: every person on board (including infants) must complete the **ED Card at [edcardaruba.aw](https://edcardaruba.aw)** within **7 days** before arrival, entering passport details, flight/vessel, accommodation (for yachts: yacht name/port). The ED Card **is not a visa** and does not by itself guarantee admission, but it is a **mandatory entry requirement** and must be shown to Immigration (IASA). Any Sustainability Fee of US$20 is integrated into the ED Card (see Fees). [edcardaruba.aw ★★★★★; visitaruba.com ★★★★★; aruba.com immigration ★★★★★]
3. **Paper forms** (optional but recommended to save time): print **Immigration 3 copies + Customs 2 copies** (PDF EN/ES/NL on arubaports.com). If not printed, they are available in the offices on the quay. [arubaports.com Download Forms ★★★★★]
4. **Approach**: about 30 minutes before arrival call **Aruba Port Control on VHF 16** (switch to 11 or 14 as instructed by the tower) and request permission to enter for clearance; follow instructions to the berth and **hoist the yellow Q flag**. [arubaports.com ★★★★★; aruba-cruisingguide.com ★★★]
5. **Berthing**: berth at the **Barcadera customs quay** (or, if diverted, at the Customs Quay / H Pier in Oranjestad on Port Control instructions). **Only the skipper goes ashore**: first **Immigration** (yellow stamp), then **Customs/Douane** (purple stamp). Crew remain on board. After signing, officers board for inspection (firearms/spear guns seized). [varaderoaruba.com ★★★; arubaports.com ★★★★★; blue-joline.com 2024 ★★★]
6. **Temporary yacht declaration (broker)**: by the next day and **no later than day 2**, a **customs broker** must start the **temporary import** procedure (see 00 — The Yacht). The broker files the declaration with Customs. [allatsea.net 2012 ★★★; aruba-cruisingguide.com ★★★]
7. **Departure (clearance out)**: always notify **Aruba Port Control on VHF 16** to agree berth and timing with customs/immigration; once formalities are complete you must **depart immediately**. [arubaports.com "CLEARANCE PROCEDURES OUT OF PORT OF ORANJESTAD" ★★★★★]

### Useful Tip (★★★)
Complete SailClear and the ED Card a few days in advance and bring **3+2 pre-filled copies** plus a separate crew list: the offices at Barcadera are small and the queue moves faster with forms ready. Also print the Sustainability Fee payment receipt if applicable.

## Stamped Paper Copy

- In Aruba there is **no** electronic "attestation" printable from a portal: the **stamped receipts** are the **paper copies** issued on the quay by **Immigration (IASA)** and **Customs** after the skipper's signature. **Keep them on board**: they serve as the **zarpe (clearance)** for the next port and as proof of temporary import. [varaderoaruba.com ★★★; arubaports.com — Customs clearance form (fields Rotation Ref. No., Date Customs, Signature Master) ★★★★★]
- Until you are stamped, **you have not legally entered**: remain on the quay/yellow area; for arrivals **after 22:00** a designated anchorage with Coast Guard permission is provided, with formalities completed the next morning. [arubaports.com ★★★★★]

## Official Ports of Entry in Aruba (official list Aruba Ports Authority ★★★★★)

Aruba has **a single port of entry for yachts**: Barcadera (with temporary diversion to Oranjestad on Port Control instructions). There is no extensive network like the French "points agréés".

Nautical notes for Barcadera (verified from three sources): stay in the **ship channel until abeam the quay** (white buoy/white tanks as reference), then turn in keeping two round bollards to port and two square bollards to starboard; draught **up to 2.1 m** can go to the end of the wooden quay keeping one beam off the quay; **3 m** only to the corner of the black tug; **>3 m call Port Control** for alternative instructions (container terminal or Oranjestad). [aruba-cruisingguide.com "To get to the customs dock" ★★★; Cruising sources aggregate Barcadera ★★★; blue-joline.com ★★★]

## Cruisers' Experiences

- Barcadera offices described as **friendly and efficient** once berthed, although the area (industrial zone, landfill to NW) is not scenic — berth with fenders and lines ready on the berthing side (usually port). ★★★ (aruba-cruisingguide.com; cruiserswiki.org)
- At Barcadera the cross-wind manoeuvre requires preparation; have **helpers ashore** ready for lines. ★★★ (blue-joline.com 2024)
- Calling **30 min before** arrival speeds up the Immigration officer's arrival (who must come down from Barcadera if you are diverted to Oranjestad). ★★★ (aruba-cruisingguide.com)
- Oranjestad Customs Quay criticised because the **quay is not designed for small boats** and is close to cruise traffic — Barcadera is preferable when available. ★★ (Cruising sources aggregate cruisers' comments)

## Penalties

- **Remaining in Aruban waters without immigration consent is an offence** ("It is considered an offense to remain in Aruban waters without the prior consent of the immigration authorities"). [arubaports.com Yachting — REQUIREMENTS ONCE IN ARUBAN WATERS ★★★★★]
- **Overstaying the granted days without an extension** may result in **refusal of future admissions** and a *blacklist* entry at border control. [visitaruba.com "Staying longer … can have negative consequences for future admission ; Admission can be refused if … blacklisted" ★★★★★]
- **Undeclared / not surrendered weapons and spear guns**: immediate seizure on inspection and possible customs penalties — **DATA MISSING** on fine amount: no published penalty table found on arubaports.com or gobierno.aw; to be verified with Customs on the quay. [varaderoaruba.com / Fonti diportiste aggregate — "rifles and spear guns will be seized" ★★★]

## To Verify Before Your Cruise

- **Immigration/Customs office hours at Barcadera / Oranjestad** (not published on arubaports.com as of 27/08/2026) — ask Port Control VHF 16/11 or Varadero Aruba (+297 588-3840).
- **Possible marina pre-notification requirement** (some local guides indicate Varadero/Wind Creek must notify authorities 3 days in advance — rule not confirmed by a government source ★★★, to be verified with your chosen marina).
- **Changes to Barcadera ↔ Oranjestad diversion**: basin works may shift the clearance point — confirm with Port Control on approach.
