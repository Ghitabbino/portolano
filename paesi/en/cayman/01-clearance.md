# 01 — Yacht Customs Clearance

**Last checked: 24/08/2026** · Main sources: Cayman Islands Customs & Border Control, Port Authority ★★★★★

## Ports of Entry (Port of Entry)

There are **two** authorised first ports of entry:

| Isola | Porto | Coordinate indicative | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Grand Cayman** | **George Town** | 19°17'N 81°23'W | Main harbour, customs and immigration H24 on call |
| **Cayman Brac** | **Scott's Bay / Charles Brac Port** | 19°43'N 79°48'W | Alternative for arrivals from the north-east |

> You must not touch any other bays or islands (including Little Cayman) before completing clearance at one of the two ports above. ★★★★★

## Entry Procedure

1. **Notice within 12 miles**: call **Port Security on VHF 16** (presidio H24) annunciando arrivo, nome barca, bandiera, provenienza and ETA. Risponde la Port Authority che istruisce sul punto of attesa. ★★★★★
2. **Q flag**: hoist the yellow flag and **no one to go ashore** fino a completamento of the formalità. Landing before clearance is strictly prohibited. ★★★★★
3. **Joint inspection on board**: salgono **Customs (with unità cinofila), Port Authority and Immigration**. Checks of ship's and crew papers, dichiarazione doganale, ispezione cambusa and gavoni. ★★★★
4. **Armi**: **consegna obbligatoria** of armi from fuoco, **spear guns and Hawaiian slings**; they are held by the authorities and returned on departure. Declare everything, including spear-guns and elastic spearguns. ★★★★★
5. **Documents to have ready**: passaporti, registro nave, lista equipaggio, clearance dell'last porto, assicurazione.

## Hours & Fees

| Item | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Customs office hours | **Mon–Fri 08:30–16:00 — Sat 08:30–12:30 — Sun closed** | CBC ★★★★★ |
| Clearance during office hours | **Free** (minimum harbour dues included) | CBC ★★★★ |
| Out-of-hours / Sunday | **USD 75** for *Special Attendance* — charged for example at Barcadere Marina | Port Authority tariff, testimonianze ★★★ |
| Permanenza barca | **No cruising permit** separato; clearance covers the stay up to the import threshold (30 days) | CBC ★★★★ |

## Departure (Outward Clearance)

- Request the **duty-free fuel chit** at outward clearance: about **CI$ 0.85/gal off diesel and CI$ 0.75/gal off petrol** (gasoline). Fuel up immediately after issue, before leaving territorial waters. ★★★
- Return of weapons held in custody.
- Notify Port Security on VHF 16.

## Penalties

| Violazione | Sanzione | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Failure to fly Q flag / landing before clearance | Fine of up to **CI$ 1.000** | Customs Act ★★★★ |
| Anchoring on coral or damaging the reef | Up to **CI$ 500,000 + 1 year imprisonment** | National Conservation Act / DOE ★★★★★ |
| Undeclared weapons | Seizure + criminal proceedings | CBC ★★★★★ |

> Anchoring on coral is a criminal offence: use only sand or public moorings → see [08 — Anchorage Pilot](08-ancoraggi.md).
