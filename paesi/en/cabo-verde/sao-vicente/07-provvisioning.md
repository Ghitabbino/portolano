# 07 — Provisioning

**Last checked: 24/08/2026** · Mindelo is the **best provisioning in the archipelago**: ultimo grande scalo prima della traversata atlantica. ★★★

## General Price Level

- Imported groceries **+20–40% vs Europe**; local (fish, seasonal fruit, bread) good value. ★★
- Wine/grogue from Santo Antão inexpensive; imported tins and dairy expensive. ★★

## Markets

- **Municipal market** + **fishermen's harbour**: very fresh fish in the morning, straight from the boats. ★★
- **Central market**: fruit/veg, spices; quality variable off-season — buy close to departure. ★★

## Bus / Aluguer (Shared Taxi Cape Verde)

| Item | Dettaglio |
|---|---|
| **Cos'è** | Minibus Toyota HiAce ("yasi") or pick-up Hilux con panche; **colectivo** — parte **quando pieno**, non a orario |
| **Fermata Mindelo** | **Praça Estrela** (centro, vicino al mercato) + fermate su Av. Marginal per Baía das Gatas/Monte Verde; chiedere "paragem de aluguer" |
| **Frequency** | Nessun orario fisso; mattina presto e tardo pomeriggio più corse; altrimenti attesa fino a riempimento (10–15 posti) |
| **Biglietto dove compro** | **A bordo, cash al conducente a fine corsa**, solo CVE, no carte; nessun sito/app |
| **Tariffe indicative** | Urbano 50–150 CVE; Mindelo–Baía das Gatas ~150 CVE; Mindelo–São Pedro ~120 CVE; inter-isola via taxi collettivo privatizzato = 10× la quota |
| **Privatizzare** | Pagando tutti i posti l'aluguer parte subito come taxi collettivo |

*Regola locale: dire "aluguer" / "aluguer coletivo" e chiedere "Quanto custa? Cada?" per evitare tariffa taxi privata.*

## Practical Tips for the Passage

- **Fruit/veg**: buy 1–2 days before departure; weekly arrivals from the mainland. ★★
- **Frozen meat**: check cold chain. ★★
- **Water**: fill up at the marina + jerry cans.
- **Folding trolley** very useful: central supermarkets 5–10 min walk from the quay.
- **Cash**: CVE essential for markets and aluguer; euros accepted but change in CVE.
