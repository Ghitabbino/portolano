# 05 — Seasonality & Weather

## Climatete

Tropical maritime, 26–30 °C all year round; dry season Dec–May. **Christmas Winds** up to 25 kn from Dec-Feb.
Admiralty Bay exposed to NE: afternoon gusts down the hills; southern anchorages (Princess Margaret, Lower Bay) are milder. ★★★

## Typical Windows

| Window | Wind | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jan–Mar | 18–25 kn | Excellent sailing, busy harbours |
| Apr–Jun | 12–18 kn | Best compromise |
| Jul–Aug | 10–15 kn | Hot and humid, low but real hurricane risk |
