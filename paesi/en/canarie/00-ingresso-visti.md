# 00 — Entry, Documents & Visas

Sheet **common** to the entire archipelago: all islands share the same entry regime. For island-specific pages → see zones in the menu.

## Status

| Item | Data |
|---|---|
| Status | **Autonomous Community of Spain**, Outermost Region (OR) of the EU · currency euro |
| Tax regime | **Outside the EU VAT area**: IGIC applies (~7% vs IVA 21%) — fuel and many goods are cheaper |
| Schengen | **INSIDE the Schengen Area** (Spain): no checks at internal borders |
| Time zone | UTC+0 winter / UTC+1 summer (**1 hour behind** mainland Spain) |
| Language | Spanish; English widely spoken in tourist marinas |

## EU Citizens — No Visa

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Visa | Not required (EU freedom of movement) |
| Documents | Valid national ID card or passport |
| Stay | No limit for EU citizens; the Canaries do not consume extra days: you are in Spain/EU |

⚠️ Schengen rules apply as normal: if arriving from outside Schengen, time spent in the area counts.

## Important Note for Arrival by Sea

- From **EU/Schengen** ports (Italy, mainland Spain, French Antilles NO — they are outside the EU customs area): no entry formalities for people.
- From **non-EU** ports (Morocco, Cape Verde, Caribbean): you must present yourself at the first official **port of entry** (Santa Cruz de Tenerife or Las Palmas de Gran Canaria) for police/customs checks.
- Between islands and to the mainland: **no formalities** (single national territory).

## The Yacht

| Yacht | Rule |
|---|---|
| **EU VAT-paid** (caso tipico: barca italiana) | EU customs territory → free movement, no cruising permit, no time limit |
| Extra-UE | Standard EU Temporary Admission: max **18 months**, renewable by exiting the customs territory |

## Vaccinations

None mandatory. Spanish healthcare standards are excellent (excellent hospitals a Santa Cruz Tenerife and Las Palmas).
