## Ancoraggi

Steep-to coast and southern bays battered by the trade wind: anchorages are few and mediocre. Marinas are the normal choice. Off Mogán in calm conditions a daytime stop is possible ⚠️ check locally.

⛔ Ferry routes around the island: frequent crossings on Las Palmas–Agaete and the south.
