7.1 30 A caricabatterie
You have chosen to fit out you boat with a 30 A caricabatterie, which allows you to charge your
batteries from the shore power.
To switch it on, switch on the interruttore magnetotermico on the 220 V panel in the galley (little caricabatterie
icon).
7.2 Serbatoi acque nere
Your Super Maramu 2000 has 2 holding tanks (one in each toilet). You must not drain them
but pump the waste in the equipped harbours.
They can be rinsed with acqua dolce added of a little bleach.
7.3 50 litri/ora dissalatore
7.3.1 Funzionamento
ATTENZIONE! Leggere attentamente the dissalatore’s manual
prima dell'uso. It contains warnings concerning your own
safety durante l'uso e la manutenzione.
Qualsiasi intervento su parti meccaniche o elettriche
on this device deve essere eseguito da personale qualificato, using
appropriate tools and protections.
The Super Maramu’s dissalatore is especially made for AMEL. It’s able to produce 50 litros /
hour (approx. 11 Imperial Gallons per hour, 14 U.S. Gallon) di acqua dolce. These values are
only for information, because the rate of desalination depends extremely on the acqua di mare
characteristics. More the temperature is low, more weaker will be the volume di acqua dolce.
Questo dispositivo funziona a 220 V tramite il caricabatterie 24 V (generator’s alternator) or on
24 V (on the main motore’s alternator or on the batteries).

7.5.2 Funzionamento del riscaldatore
Before working the heater, be sure the outside air inlet
(in the cockpit, inside of the ropes stock location at the foot of the mizzen mast) is not
closed.
The fuel heater works switching on the actuating unit right to the dissalatore panel. 4
temperature settings, a ventilation and an OFF position are available. The warmed air is
dispatched in :
- the saloon,
- the fore cabin,
- the aft cabin.
The power is approximately 4800 W at maximum speed.
Refer to the builder’s manual, section “Technical description” for more information.
Each heating outlet can be closed independently of the other.
Remark : in the fourth position, the consumption of this heater is approximately 0,58 litros of
fuel per hour (0.13 Imperial Gallon, 0.15 U.S. Gallon)

the plug called “220 V depuis transfo sur quai 110 V”,

7.19 24 / 220 V inverter
PERICOLO! Leggere attentamente the inverter’s manual before
use. Contiene avvertenze riguardanti la vostra sicurezza
durante l'uso e la manutenzione.
The user’s manual gives some security information that
must be respected for a secure use of this appliance.
Refer to it before continuing.
Qualsiasi intervento su parti meccaniche o elettriche
on this device deve essere eseguito da personale qualificato, using
appropriate tools and protections.
An inverter changes a 24 V DC voltage (the batteries) to an alternating 220 V voltage. This
type of appliance doesn’t allow to use “big consumers” (dish washer, washer / dryer
machine).
If we use the microwave oven with the inverter, the interruttore magnetotermico will switch it off 3 or 4
minutes later : it’s normal. The functioning time of the microwave oven is intentionally limited
to the necessary time for heating a cup of coffee for example, without starting the power
generator.

7.21 Elica shaft alternator
You have chosen to equip your Super Maramu 2000 with a propeller shaft alternator. It gives
up to 20 A, and is really efficient from 7 knots. It can be unclutched with an electrical shutoff
located close to the transmission.
Because of safety rules, don’t connect the propeller shaft alternator cruising with the motore.
The control switch is located in the cockpit, close to the voltmeter and the temperature
indicator.
To use the propeller shaft alternator with the sails :
- start the main motore without clutching it, to make the hydraulic brake free,
- clutch in the rear sense (to spread the propeller’s blades), and then come back
in the neutral position,
- excite the alternator switching on (in the up position) the control switch,
- stop the motore.
- the indicator becomes green (the red indicator lights if the propeller’s rotation
isn’t fast enough),
To stop the propeller shaft alternator, stop the excitation (which allows the hydraulic brake to
close up) switching in the lower position the control switch.
