Shaft seal cleaning – Re-installation of the back part of 
the pump body
This is the new shaft seal – note 
the left grafite part with mirror 
finished surface and the right part 
which is rotating with the shaft
Deep the grafite part in water and 
then slide it on the shaft keeping 
the mirror finished surface towards 
you
Deep the rotating part in water and 
then slide it on the shaft. Push it by 
end. It is important that this part 
goes back on the shaft.
